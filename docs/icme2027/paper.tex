\title{\method: Selective Information Filtering with P/E-Core Transport for Real-Time Style Transfer}

\author{\IEEEauthorblockN{Anonymous ICME Submission}
\IEEEauthorblockA{Paper ID: ****}}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
\emph{More capacity can preserve the wrong information. Effective style transfer requires selective information removal throughout the entire generative pipeline.} Across $300+$ controlled runs on a 750-task benchmark, we uncover three counter-intuitive pathologies where preserving raw capacity actively harms stylization: spatial features leak $91.33\%$ source style through 2nd-order Gram correlations; scaling a self-paired latent flow by $260\times$ improves unconditional generation yet degrades cross-style editing due to Posterior Source-Lock; and a full SD-VAE decoder retracts stylized latents toward natural textures. We present \textbf{\method} (\textbf{C}ontinuous \textbf{L}atent \textbf{E}diting and \textbf{A}bstraction \textbf{V}elocity \textbf{E}ngine), a real-time framework deploying targeted information filters across conditioning, trajectory, and decoding under a single knob $s\in[0,1.50]$. First, a convolutional \textbf{Stiefel Polar Quotient} clamps feature Gram matrices to $I_{32}$, suppressing 5-way style leakage from $91.33\%$ to $20.67\%$ (chance floor) without DINOv2 backbones. Second, a CPU-inspired two-tier \textbf{Performance--Efficiency (P/E)-Core} engine dispatches regimes by the user's desired efficiency--abstraction budget rather than input-dependent MoE routing: a 1-NFE \textbf{\methodecore} handles structure-faithful editing ($s\le 0.75$) with exact $s=0$ identity and emits a $1\,\text{ms}$ draft, while a 6-step \textbf{\methodpcore} strips high-frequency skips ($16\times 16$ only) to break source-lock at $s\ge 0.80$. Third, pruning $78.5\%$ of decoder channels (\textbf{\methoddec}) blocks texture retraction, raising style accuracy by $+4.49\text{pp}$ at $3.15\times$ speed. \textbf{\method} spans the full style--content frontier at $18.2\text{--}33.1\,\text{img/s}$ cold-start RGB$\to$RGB---$550\times\text{--}1{,}000\times$ faster than StyleGallery under blind cross-architecture evaluation.
\end{abstract}

Interactive artistic authoring requires a real-time engine that sweeps smoothly across the full style--content spectrum, from subtle palette adaptation to bold brushstroke reorganization. Yet modern style transfer remains split into two incompatible extremes (Fig.~\ref{fig:page1}): billion-parameter zero-shot diffusion editors~\cite{gao2024styleshot,chung2024style,he2026stylegallery,seedream2025} require $5\text{--}30.4\,\text{s/img}$ of iterative inversion or attention optimization, whereas lightweight feed-forward networks~\cite{deng2022stytr2,hong2023aespa,park2020cut,liu2024samam,afifi2021histogan} either preserve contours by barely shifting style ($<40\%$ CSD Top-1) or collapse scene geometry under global moment matching.

Across $300+$ controlled runs on a 750-task benchmark, we uncover a counter-intuitive cause behind this impasse: \emph{more capacity can preserve the wrong information}. Scaling a self-paired network amplifies the very reconstruction bias that stylization must overcome. Specifically, raw source-style signals backflow through three distinct channels of a latent generative pipeline (Fig.~\ref{fig:arch}, empirically verified in Fig.~\ref{fig:paradoxes}):
(i)~\textbf{Conditioning-Space Style Leakage:} raw spatial tokens encode off-diagonal channel correlations whose 2nd-order Gram matrices reveal source style with $91.33\%$ linear accuracy, prompting conditional generators to copy source brushwork;
(ii)~\textbf{Trajectory-Space Posterior Source-Lock (Breaking the Single-Core Conflict):} high-content editing ($s\le 0.75$) is an injective $1$-to-$1$ map that must retain $64\times 64$ contours with zero drift at $s=0$, whereas strong abstraction ($s\ge 0.80$) is a stochastic $1$-to-many synthesis task that must erase source micro-edges. Scaling a single self-paired flow~\cite{lipman2022flow,meng2021sdedit} by $260\times$ ($0.13\text{M}\!\to\!34.2\text{M}$) lifts unconditional generation ($\tau_0=0$) from $20.0\%$ to $49.3\%$ while degrading cross-style SDEdit ($\tau_0=0.7$) from $10.67\%$ to $\mathbf{6.67\%}$ ($59\%$ locked to source style); and
(iii)~\textbf{Decoding-Space Texture Retraction:} over-parameterized $49.5\text{M}$ SD-VAE decoders~\cite{rombach2022high} retain natural-image high-frequency priors that pull stylized latents back toward unedited textures.

We propose \textbf{\method} (\textbf{C}ontinuous \textbf{L}atent \textbf{E}diting and \textbf{A}bstraction \textbf{V}elocity \textbf{E}ngine)---named for its dual physical role of \emph{cleaving away} unwanted source style across all three channels while \emph{cleaving closely} to source geometry in high-content regimes. First, \textbf{Filter~I (Conditioning)} replaces heavy ViT extractors with a $\cnxParams$ ConvNeXt-Tiny Stage-3 Stiefel Polar Quotient ($Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}}$), clamping sample Gram matrices to $I_{32}$ ($91.33\%\!\to\!20.67\%$ chance floor) alongside a 417-D $K=8$ sub-school style bank. Second, \textbf{Filter~II (Trajectory)} introduces a two-tier \textbf{Performance--Efficiency (P/E)-Core} transport operator ($\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{P/E}}$) modeled after hybrid CPUs rather than Mixture-of-Experts (MoE): instead of learning input-dependent token routing across homogeneous experts, a deterministic \textbf{Hybrid Director} selects between two structurally asymmetric cores based solely on the user's chosen abstraction--efficiency regime $s\in[0,1.50]$. For $s\le 0.75$, 1-NFE \textbf{\methodecore} ($\ecoreParams$) preserves gated $64^2$ contours with exact $s=0$ identity ($\max\lvert\hat{z}-z_s\rvert=0.0$) and emits a $1\,\text{ms}$ draft $z_{\mathrm{E}}$; for $s\ge 0.80$, 6-step \textbf{\methodpcore} ($\pcoreParams$) starts from $z_{\mathrm{E}}$, discards $64^2/32^2$ structural skips ($16\times 16$ bottleneck only), and trains with $15\%$ in-domain reversed real-painting pairs (\texttt{Pool C}) to break source-lock. Third, \textbf{Filter~III (Decoding)} applies $\decPrune$ Taylor channel pruning to SD-VAE (\textbf{\methoddec}, $\decParams$), blocking texture retraction ($+4.49\text{pp}$ Top-1 at $\decSpeedup$ speed). \emph{\textbf{\method} is compact not because it sacrifices generative capacity, but because it removes the capacity to preserve the wrong information.}

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Lightweight Feed-Forward Style Transfer.}
Neural style transfer progressed from iterative Gram matching~\cite{gatys2016image,simonyan2014vgg} to feed-forward statistics alignment (AdaIN~\cite{huang2017arbitrary}, WCT~\cite{li2017universal}), attention/transformer modules (StyTr$^2$~\cite{deng2022stytr2}, AesPA-Net~\cite{hong2023aespa}, AesFA~\cite{kwon2024aesfa}), unpaired contrastive translation (CUT~\cite{park2020cut}), histogram transfer (Hist2Style~\cite{afifi2021histogan}), and state-space networks (SaMam~\cite{liu2024samam}, SaMST~\cite{liu2024samst}). Viewed through information filtering, global moment matching indiscriminately erases spatial layout, whereas conservative translators retain unwhitened source skips that lock onto source strokes.

\textbf{Diffusion Stylizers, Latent Bridges, and Decoder Pruning.}
Large pretrained diffusion and multimodal editors~\cite{meng2021sdedit,zhang2023inst,ye2023ipadapter,hertz2024style,chung2024style,gao2024styleshot,he2026stylegallery,seedream2025} rely on billion-parameter priors and $5\text{--}30.4\,\text{s/img}$ sampling via attention injection (StyleID~\cite{chung2024style}, StyleShot~\cite{gao2024styleshot}), multimodal decoding (Seedream~4.5~\cite{seedream2025}), or 150-step DINOv2-guided inversion and matching (StyleGallery~\cite{he2026stylegallery}). Latent flow bridges~\cite{lipman2022flow,liu2022rectified,chadebec2025lbm,kim2024latentsb} and spatial adapters~\cite{zhang2023controlnet,park2019spade} reduce step counts yet suffer Posterior Source-Lock under self-paired $64\times 64$ conditioning. Unlike reconstruction-driven VAE pruning~\cite{sadat2024litevae,lin2023favae,molchanov2019importance}, \textbf{\methoddec} uses structured channel pruning to remove source-retracting texture priors.

\section{Methodology: Three-Stage Information Filtering}
\label{sec:method}

Given an unlabeled source image $x_s \in [0,1]^{3\times H\times W}$ with VAE latent $z_s = \mathcal{E}(x_s) \in \R^{4\times 64\times 64}$ ($H=W=512$) and target movement $y_t \in \{1,\dots,5\}$ under single knob $s \in [0, 1.50]$, \textbf{\method} synthesizes $\hat{x}_{s\to y_t}(s) = \mathcal{D}_\star(\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{P/E}}(z_s, \Pi_\perp^{\mathrm{cnx}}(x_s), \Pi_\parallel^{\mathrm{cnx}}(x_s, y_t); s))$ via three sequential information filters (Fig.~\ref{fig:arch}).

\subsection{Filter I (Conditioning): Stiefel Polar Quotient \& Style Bank}
\label{sec:quotient}

\textbf{1) Multi-Scale Luminance Structure ($C_{64}, Y_{16}$):}
From ITU-R BT.601 grayscale luminance $Y(x)=w_Y^\top x$ downsampled to $Y_{64}$ with local residual $\Delta_k Y=Y_{64}-\mathcal{A}_k(Y_{64})$ ($k\in\{5,13\}$), gradient-safe Local Contrast Normalization is
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{LCN}_k(Y_{64}) = \tfrac{1}{3}\mathrm{clip}\!\left(\frac{\Delta_k Y}{\sqrt{\max(\mathcal{A}_k(\Delta_k Y^2),\delta_0)} + \varepsilon_k},-3,3\right),
\label{eq:lcn}
\end{equation}
($\delta_0=10^{-8}$), stacked with 95th-percentile Sobel and RMS Laplacian maps into $C_{64}(x)\in[-1,1]^{4\times 64\times 64}$ for \textbf{\methodecore}, and pooled to $16\times 16$ $\mathrm{LCN}_3(Y_{16})\in[-1,1]^{1\times 16\times 16}$ (32px blocks, free of micro-strokes) for \textbf{\methodpcore}.

\textbf{2) ConvNeXt-Stage3 Stiefel Polar Quotient $Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}}$:}
We truncate an ImageNet-pretrained ConvNeXt-Tiny backbone at Stage~3 (\texttt{features[:6]}, $\cnxParams$ params, $D=384$ at stride~$16$). Feeding $256\times 256$ grayscale $[Y,Y,Y]$ yields spatial tokens $P_{\mathrm{cnx}}\in\R^{384\times 256}$ in $\mathbf{0.985\,\text{ms/img}}$ (\texttt{b=16}), avoiding both heavy ViT latency and DINOv2 evaluator coupling. Because standard channel whitening retains off-diagonal correlations that leak $91.33\%$ source style via 2nd-order Gram matrices, we project onto the Stiefel manifold:

\begin{proposition}[Stiefel Polar Quotient \& Zero 2nd-Order Gram Leakage]
\label{prop:stiefel}
Let $W_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}} \in \R^{384\times 32}$ be the principal semantic basis fit on training grayscale tokens. For spatially centered projection $Z = (W_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}})^\top P_{\mathrm{cnx}}(I_N - \frac{1}{N}\mathbf{1}\mathbf{1}^\top) \in \R^{32\times N}$ ($N=256$) with compact SVD $Z = U \Sigma V^\top$ ($U\in O(32), V^\top V = I_{32}$), the Stiefel Polar Quotient
\begin{equation}
Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}}(x) = \sqrt{N}\, U V^\top \in \R^{32\times 16\times 16}
\label{eq:stiefel-q}
\end{equation}
is the unique Frobenius-nearest tensor to $Z$ satisfying exact zero spatial mean ($Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}}\mathbf{1} \equiv \mathbf{0}$) and exact identity sample Gram matrix ($\frac{1}{N} Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}} (Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}})^\top \equiv I_{32}$).
\end{proposition}
On 300 holdout paintings, $Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}}$ achieves a max Gram numerical error of $1.95\times 10^{-4}$ and drops 5-way linear Gram style predictability from $91.33\%$ to $\mathbf{20.67\%}$ ($20.0\%$ chance floor).

\textbf{3) 417-D $K=8$ Sub-School Style Bank:}
To supply clean target brushwork statistics, we form $f_{417}(x) = [\text{Oklab}_{27}(x) \;\|\; \text{Haar}_{6}(x) \;\|\; \mu_{\mathrm{RGB}}^{\mathrm{ConvNeXt\text{-}S3}}(x)] \in \R^{417}$, combining 27-D Oklab color moments/quantiles/covariances, 6-D 3-level Haar log-energy ratios, and the 384-D RGB ConvNeXt-Stage3 spatial mean. Within each movement's $k$-NN-30 core manifold, spherical $k$-means partitions exemplars into $K=8$ sub-schools with centroids $\{u_{y_t,k}^{\mathrm{cnx}}\}_{k=1}^8 \subset \R^{417}$ and $4\times 4$-pooled grayscale scene descriptors $\{s_{y_t,k}^{\mathrm{cnx}}\}_{k=1}^8 \subset \R^{6144}$, routing each source scene via $k^\star = \arg\max_k \langle s(x_s), s_{y_t,k}^{\mathrm{cnx}}\rangle$ in $0.025\,\text{ms}$.

\subsection{Filter II (Trajectory): Source-Lock \& P/E-Core Transport}
\label{sec:transport}

In self-paired flow matching~\cite{lipman2022flow}, velocity field $v_\theta(z_\tau,\tau,y)$ trains on $z_\tau = (1-\tau)\epsilon + \tau z$ and edits via SDEdit~\cite{meng2021sdedit} from $z_{\tau_0} = (1-\tau_0)\epsilon + \tau_0 z_s$ ($z_s\sim\Prob_{y_s}$) under $y_t \neq y_s$.

\begin{proposition}[Posterior Source-Lock]
\label{prop:sourcelock}
With label dropout $p_{\mathrm{drop}}\in(0,1)$, the Bayes-optimal velocity at $(z_{\tau_0},\tau_0,y_t)$ is $\frac{\hat{z}_{\tau_0}(y_t)-z_{\tau_0}}{1-\tau_0}$, where the posterior expectation under $\tilde{\Prob}_{y_t}=(1-\eta)\Prob_{y_t}+\eta\sum_k\pi_k\Prob_k$ is
\begin{equation}
\hat{z}_{\tau_0}(y_t) = \frac{\int_{\mathcal{Z}} z \, \exp\!\Big(-\frac{\norm{z_{\tau_0} - \tau_0 z}_2^2}{2(1-\tau_0)^2}\Big) d\tilde{\Prob}_{y_t}(z)}{\int_{\mathcal{Z}} \exp\!\Big(-\frac{\norm{z_{\tau_0} - \tau_0 z}_2^2}{2(1-\tau_0)^2}\Big) d\tilde{\Prob}_{y_t}(z)}.
\label{eq:posterior-lock}
\end{equation}
For $\Delta_{s\to t}(z_s)=\mathrm{dist}(z_s,\mathrm{supp}(\Prob_{y_t}))^2$, whenever $\frac{\tau_0^2\Delta_{s\to t}(z_s)}{2(1-\tau_0)^2} \gg \log\frac{1-\eta}{\eta\pi_{y_s}}$, the Gaussian kernel concentrates exponentially on $z_s\in\mathrm{supp}(\Prob_{y_s})$, yielding $\norm{\hat{z}_{\tau_0}(y_t)-z_s}_2=\mathcal{O}(1-\tau_0)$ regardless of $y_t$.
\end{proposition}
Prop.~\ref{prop:sourcelock} shows why scaling a single self-paired network worsens cross-style SDEdit: high $\tau_0$ locks the trajectory onto $z_s$, whereas lowering $\tau_0$ without a pre-shifted draft destroys layout. To break this single-core conflict, we design a two-tier \textbf{P/E-Core} transport engine over an orthogonal luma--chroma split $P_Y = \hat{w}_Y\hat{w}_Y^\top$, $P_C = I_4 - P_Y$ ($Y_{64}\approx w_{\mathrm{luma}}^\top z + b_{\mathrm{luma}}$). Crucially, unlike Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures that route tokens dynamically by input content across homogeneous experts, our Hybrid Director functions like a hybrid CPU scheduler: it exposes \emph{two fixed, asymmetric compute tiers} selected deterministically by the user's desired efficiency--abstraction operating point $s\in[0,1.50]$.

\textbf{1) \methodecore{} ($\ecoreParams$, 1-NFE, $s \in [0, 0.75]$ --- Contour Retention \& Draft Seeding):}
In the high-content tier, \textbf{\methodecore} runs a single feed-forward pass ($0.986\,\text{ms/img}$ core, $\ecoreColdBatchMs$ cold RGB$\to$RGB) via a gated hybrid U-Net:
\begin{equation}
z_{\mathrm{E}}(x_s, y_t; \lambda) \!=\! z_0^\perp(z_s, y_t; \lambda) + \lambda \Delta_{\theta_{\mathrm{E}}}\!\big(z_s, \gamma_{\mathrm{skip}}(\lambda) C_{64}, Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}}, y_t\big),
\label{eq:ecore}
\end{equation}
where $z_0^\perp(z_s,y_t;0)\equiv z_s$ and skip gate $\gamma_{\mathrm{skip}}(\lambda)=\max(0, 1-0.8\lambda)$ gradually releases $64^2$ contours. At $s=0$, Eq.~\eqref{eq:ecore} yields exact mathematical identity ($\max\lvert\hat{z}-z_s\rvert=0.0$). Above $s=0.75$, \textbf{\methodecore} serves as a $1\,\text{ms}$ draft generator $z_{\mathrm{E}}(\lambda_{\mathrm{draft}}(s))$ that pre-shifts palette and coarse brushwork out of the source attraction basin.

\textbf{2) \methodpcore{} ($\pcoreParams$, 6-Step DPM-2M Flow, $s \in [0.80, 1.50]$ --- Micro-Edge Removal \& Pool C):}
In the abstraction tier, \textbf{\methodpcore} initializes at $z_{\tau_0} = (1-\tau_0(s))\epsilon + \tau_0(s) z_{\mathrm{E}}(x_s, y_t; \lambda_{\mathrm{draft}}(s))$ ($\tau_0(1.50)=0$) and integrates 6 steps of 2nd-order DPM-Solver++ (\texttt{dpm2m}) with 4-branch decoupled CFG:
\begin{equation}
v_{\mathrm{CFG}} = v_{00} + w_c(v_{0c}\!-\!v_{00}) + w_s(v_{sc}\!-\!v_{0c}) + w_{sp}(v_{s0}\!-\!v_{00}).
\label{eq:pcore-cfg}
\end{equation}
\textbf{\methodpcore} \emph{removes all $64^2/32^2$ structural skips}, injecting $(\mathrm{LCN}_3(Y_{16}), \gamma_{\mathrm{layout}}(s) Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}})$ solely at the $16\times 16$ bottleneck while modulating $32^2/64^2$ blocks by $u_{y_t,k^\star}^{\mathrm{cnx}}$. During training ($3{,}500$ steps, $27.1\,\text{min}$ on one RTX~4090), each batch mixes $85\%$ $k$-NN-30 core paintings with $15\%$ \textbf{in-domain reversed real-painting pairs (\texttt{Pool C})}---pairing real ground-truth targets ($z_{\mathrm{real}}, y_t$, 600 ER + 600 Uki) with de-styled structural conditions extracted from synthetic cross-style sources---augmented with high-$\tau$ pixel-space SWD supervision through \textbf{\methoddec}.

\subsection{Filter III (Decoding): Spectral-Bottleneck Pruning (\methoddec)}
\label{sec:decoder}

Over-parameterized synthesis filters in the $49.49\text{M}$ SD-VAE decoder retain natural-image high-frequency priors that pull stylized latents back toward unedited textures. Applying 4-stream Taylor importance pruning~\cite{molchanov2019importance} ($\mathcal{I}(g)=\lvert\sum W\,\partial\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{rec}}/\partial W\rvert$) removes $\decPrune$ of channels down to widths $[64,128,192,256]$ (\textbf{\methoddec}, $\decParams$), creating a spectral bottleneck that blocks texture retraction ($\decGain\text{--}\decClosedGain$ Top-1) at $\decSpeedup$ speed.

\subsection{Redefining the Quality--Efficiency Frontier}

Table~\ref{tab:main}, Fig.~\ref{fig:page1}, and Fig.~\ref{fig:qual} establish three primary empirical findings:

\textbf{Finding 1: Real-Time Editing No Longer Requires Weak Stylization.}
Running in $\ecoreColdBatchMs$ cold RGB$\to$RGB ($\ecoreColdBatchFps$; $\ecoreHotBatchMs / \ecoreHotBatchFps$ hot interactive) with $98.0\%\text{--}98.7\%$ Same150 identity retention, 1-NFE \textbf{\methodecore} outperforms every Tier~1 baseline and commercial high-content editor across all metrics simultaneously. Notably, at $s=0.60$, \textbf{\methodecore} surpasses commercial Seedream~4.5 ($20.0\,\text{s/img}$) on \emph{every} quality axis---higher content fidelity ($0.7781$ vs.\ $0.7343$ DINO-C), stronger style similarity ($0.4407$ vs.\ $0.3885$ DINO-S), higher target accuracy ($38.83\%$ vs.\ $37.30\%$ CSD Top-1), and lower distortion ($0.4322$ vs.\ $0.5202$ LPIPS)---at $\mathbf{660\times}$ higher throughput. Pushing to $s=0.75$ ($\sZeroSeventyFiveC$ C, $\sZeroSeventyFiveS$ S, $\sZeroSeventyFiveTop$ Top-1) likewise outperforms StyTr$^2$ ($39.5\%$, $3.0\,\text{s}$) and $15\text{B}$ Qwen-Image-2.1T ($42.2\%$, $16.1\,\text{s}$) by $+3.0\text{--}5.7\text{pp}$ Top-1 at $100\times\text{--}530\times$ higher speed.

\textbf{Finding 2: Strong Stylization Does Not Require Billion-Parameter Generators.}
Above $s=0.75$, 6-step \textbf{\methodpcore} ($\pcoreColdBatchMs$ cold RGB$\to$RGB, $\pcoreColdBatchFps$) bridges smoothly into mid-to-extreme painterly abstraction. At the balanced $s=0.85$ point ($\sZeroEightyFiveC$ DINO-C), \textbf{\methodpcore} beats $950\text{M}$ StyleGallery ($30.4\,\text{s/img}$) by $\mathbf{+9.83\text{pp}}$ on independent CSD Top-1 ($62.33\%$ vs.\ $52.50\%$), while standalone 1-NFE \textbf{\methodecore} ($s=1.00$) beats it by $\mathbf{+14.67\text{pp}}$ ($67.17\%$) at $\mathbf{550\times\text{--}1{,}000\times}$ higher cold-start throughput. StyleGallery achieves higher DINO-S at $C\!\approx\!0.61$ ($0.5604$ vs.\ $0.5266$) due to its 150-step optimization directly in DINOv2 space and stronger texture erasure on \emph{Minimalism} (whereas \textbf{\method} preserves contours on the hard Minimalism case, Fig.~\ref{fig:qual}b, while already reaching $0.560\text{--}0.658$ DINO-S on figurative movements). In the strong-style regime ($s=1.00\text{--}1.50$), \textbf{\methodpcore} reaches $\mathbf{0.6054}$ DINO-S ($0.5461$ Top-5 Mean) and $\mathbf{80.17\%}$ Top-1, surpassing both StyleGallery ($0.5604$) and $2.4\text{B}$ StyleShot ($0.5750$) at $450\times\text{--}550\times$ their throughput.

\textbf{Finding 3: Selective Removal Improves Transfer, Not Just Efficiency.}
As visualized in Fig.~\ref{fig:paradoxes} and quantified in Table~\ref{tab:ablation}, removing each source-style backflow channel directly raises transfer quality:
(1)~\emph{Filter~I (Conditioning):} In the 6-arm $Q_{32}$ causal test on Test750, Stiefel $Q_{32}$ strips 5-way Gram style leakage ($91.33\%\!\to\!20.67\%$) while contributing $+0.0625\text{--}0.0742$ DINO-C ($95\%$ CI excludes 0) and zero style shift ($\Delta\text{S}\le 0.0028$, NS) at $\tau_0=0.75$; zeroing $Q_{32}$ at $\tau_0=0$ collapses both content ($-0.0853$) and composition ($-0.0877$).
(2)~\emph{Filter~II (Trajectory):} Under counterfactual wrong target IDs, \textbf{\method}'s Top-1 drops to $1.17\%\text{--}2.17\%$, confirming $+60.2\text{--}78.0\text{pp}$ causal target specificity (vs.\ $-0.7\text{ to }+9.8\text{pp}$ for unfiltered baselines).
(3)~\emph{Filter~III (Decoding):} On identical flow latents, pruning $\decPrune$ of SD-VAE channels (\textbf{\methoddec}) lifts Top-1 by $+4.49\text{pp}$ over WikiArt-finetuned full SD-VAE ($60.33\%$ vs.\ $55.83\%$, $p<0.05$) while cutting LPIPS ($0.602$ vs.\ $0.666$) at $\decSpeedup$ speed.

\textbf{End-to-End Efficiency (Table~\ref{tab:system}A).}
Because ConvNeXt-S3 ($0.985\,\text{ms}$) and 1-NFE \textbf{\methodecore} ($0.986\,\text{ms}$) each run in under $1\,\text{ms/img}$ at \texttt{b=16}, the frozen VAE encoder ($15.63\,\text{ms}$) and pruned \textbf{\methoddec} ($12.51\,\text{ms}$) dominate cold-start RGB$\to$RGB latency ($93\%$ for \textbf{\methodecore} at $\ecoreColdBatchFps$; $51\%$ for \textbf{\methodpcore} at $\pcoreColdBatchFps$). In interactive sessions where a source image is encoded once and $s$ is swept continuously, throughput rises to $\ecoreHotBatchFps$ (\textbf{\methodecore}) and $\pcoreHotBatchFps$ (\textbf{\methodpcore}). Total online hot parameters are $\hotTotalParams$ ($\coldTotalParams$ cold including the frozen encoder)---under $1/10$ the footprint of $\text{SD-1.5}+\text{DINOv2}$ pipelines.

\textbf{Beyond DINO-Dependent Stylization (Table~\ref{tab:system}B).}
Paired cluster-bootstrap comparisons against DINOv2-conditioned P/E-Core twins confirm that \textbf{\method}'s style quality is backbone-independent: at $s=1.00$ and $1.25$, \emph{DINO-S} ($\Delta=+0.0023, -0.0015$), \emph{Style Affinity} ($\Delta=-0.0002$), and \emph{CSD Top-1} ($\Delta=-1.83\text{pp}, -1.67\text{pp}$) all have $95\%$ CIs spanning zero. Replacing DINOv2 with ConvNeXt-S3 shifts \emph{DINO-C} by a uniform $-0.023\text{ to }-0.032$ even when independent \emph{LPIPS} is tied or slightly better ($\Delta=-0.0038$), quantifying a $\sim 0.025$ scale offset from ViT generator--evaluator homology. At the $\tau_0=0$ endpoint ($s=1.50$), the residual DINO-S gap ($0.6054$ vs.\ $0.6379$) localizes to \emph{Rococo} ($0.5387$ vs.\ $0.6390$), where grayscale ConvNeXt $4\times 4$ scene descriptors exhibit cosine hubness collapse onto cluster $c_7$ ($45.8\%$ of 120 tasks, $H/\ln 8=0.7102$ vs.\ $0.8687$, scoring $0.4879$ DINO-S vs.\ $0.6614 / 100\%$ Top-1 on non-hub $c_3$).

\textbf{Toward Open-Domain Stylization.}
\textbf{\method} amortizes a 5-movement repertoire to achieve real-time cold-start throughput under blind evaluation. Extending Sinkhorn-balanced ConvNeXt sub-school routing and \texttt{Pool C} reversed-pair training to open-domain 1-shot arbitrary references is a direct next step.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We showed that \emph{more capacity can preserve the wrong information} and countered source-style backflow across conditioning, trajectory, and decoding with \textbf{\method}. Combining a convolutional Stiefel Polar Quotient ($91.33\%\!\to\!20.67\%$ Gram probe), a two-tier CPU-like \textbf{P/E-Core} transport engine (\textbf{\methodecore} 1-NFE + \textbf{\methodpcore} 6-step flow with $16\times 16$-only injection and \texttt{Pool C} reversed pairs), and a $78.5\%$-pruned spectral decoder (\textbf{\methoddec}, $+4.49\text{pp}$ at $3.15\times$ speed), \textbf{\method} spans the full style--content frontier at $18.2\text{--}33.1\,\text{img/s}$ cold-start RGB$\to$RGB ($550\times\text{--}1{,}000\times$ faster than StyleGallery).
