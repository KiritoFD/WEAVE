\title{\method: Breaking Source-Style Lock to Redefine the Content--Style--Speed Frontier}

\author{\IEEEauthorblockN{Anonymous ICME Submission}
\IEEEauthorblockA{Paper ID: ****}}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Scaling a self-paired latent flow by $260\times$ ($0.13\text{M}\!\to\!34.2\text{M}$) improves unconditional generation ($20.0\%\!\to\!49.3\%$), yet makes cross-style transfer worse ($10.67\%\!\to\!6.67\%$). We identify \emph{Source-Style Lock} as a fundamental failure mode of reconstruction-oriented latent transport: self-paired trajectories, diagonal feature whitening, and over-parameterized decoders retain source-domain texture cues that pull edited latents back toward the input style. We propose \textbf{\method} (\emph{Projected Invariant Style Mapping}), an orthogonal quotient--fiber framework $\mathcal{D}_\star \circ \mathcal{T}_{s\to\tau} \circ (\Pi_\perp,\Pi_\parallel)$ that blocks source-style backflow while steering transport along a source-label-free target chord through a pruned spectral-bottleneck decoder. Across a 750-task benchmark on a single consumer RTX~3060, \textbf{\method} redefines the content--style--compute frontier: its high-content regime reaches $\hcDinoC$ DINO-C and $\hcDinoS$ DINO-S ($\hcLpips$ LPIPS, $\hcBatchMs$), its generative flow reaches $\flowBalTopOne$ target accuracy and $\flowBalDinoS$ DINO-S (surpassing diffusion cross-attention StyleGallery at $30.4\,\text{s/img}$), and its real-time regime executes in $\fastMs$ cached ($\fastBatchEtoEMs$ end-to-end RGB$\to$RGB; $\fastConsumerFps$ under $3.9\,\text{GB}$ VRAM).
\end{abstract}

Why should artistic style transfer require billion-parameter diffusion backbones and tens of seconds of per-image optimization when a compact latent model operates at $1/8$ spatial resolution? In principle, encoding images into a frozen variational autoencoder (VAE)~\cite{rombach2022high} should reduce conditional stylization—rendering an unlabeled input $x_s$ in target style $\tau$ while preserving scene geometry—to millisecond-scale latent transport. In practice, existing multimedia generators split into two incompatible computational paradigms (Fig.~\ref{fig:page1}): diffusion-heavy editors~\cite{gao2024styleshot,chung2024style,hertz2024style,he2026stylegallery} achieve strong stylization only via $5\text{--}30\,\text{s/img}$ of iterative inversion or attention optimization, whereas conventional lightweight translators~\cite{deng2022stytr2,hong2023aespa,park2020cut,liu2024samam,afifi2021histogan} cluster in weak-stylization or high-distortion regimes.

Across $300+$ controlled runs on a 750-task benchmark, we trace this barrier to \textbf{Source-Style Lock}: trajectories, feature normalizers, and decoders trained for \emph{self-reconstruction} preserve source-domain mutual information $I(\cdot\,;Y_s\mid C)$ that actively resists cross-style transport. Crucially, our experiments challenge three prevailing assumptions in generative style transfer (Fig.~\ref{fig:paradoxes}):

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Scaling Does Not Solve Style Transfer ($260\times$ Scaling Paradox, Fig.~\ref{fig:paradoxes}a):} Scaling a self-paired rectified flow~\cite{lipman2022flow,meng2021sdedit} by $260\times$ ($0.13\text{M}\!\to\!34.2\text{M}$) lifts pure noise generation ($\tau_0=0$) Top-1 from $20.0\%$ to $49.3\%$, yet monotonically \emph{degrades} cross-style SDEdit ($\tau_0=0.7$) from $10.67\%$ to $\mathbf{6.67\%}$ ($59\%$ predicted back as source style $y_s$). Even $\tau_0=0$ flows conditioned on $64\times 64$ chrominance-free edges stall at $18.67\%$ because self-paired training ($x_s\mapsto x_s$) inverts 2D stroke cadence back into $y_s$.
\item \textbf{Reconstruction Fidelity Can Fight Stylization (Fig.~\ref{fig:paradoxes}b):} More capacity can preserve the wrong information not only in the flow, but also in the decoder. Full $49.5\text{M}$ SD-VAE synthesis filters memorize source-domain texture priors; pruning $\decPrune$ of channels (\textbf{\methoddec}, $\decParams$) yields a $\decSpeedup$ speedup while \emph{increasing} cross-style Top-1 by \textbf{\decGain} ($p<0.01$).
\item \textbf{Evaluation Mirages Obscure True Specificity (Fig.~\ref{fig:paradoxes}c):} Standard diagonal feature whitening retains $92.33\%$ off-diagonal Gram style leakage, while unanchored post-hoc scalers score \emph{higher} under counterfactual \emph{wrong} target labels than true ones ($13.0\%$ vs.\ $12.3\%$).
\end{enumerate}

To break Source-Style Lock without billion-parameter diffusion or test-time source labels, we propose \textbf{\method} (\emph{Projected Invariant Style Mapping}), built on \textbf{Orthogonal Quotient--Fiber Duality} $(\Pi_\perp,\Pi_\parallel)$. While $\Pi_\perp(x_s)$ projects onto chrominance-free contrast and a Stiefel polar semantic layout with exact zero 2nd-order sample Gram leakage ($20.00\%$ 5-way Gram probe, uniform chance), its complement $\Pi_\parallel(x_s)$ collects the discarded color quantiles and DINO spatial moments ($97.33\%$ style accuracy) to form a \emph{source-label-free target chord} $e(x_s\!\to\!\tau)=u_\tau-\phi(\Pi_\parallel(x_s))$. Splitting $\R^4=\mathrm{span}(\hat{w}_Y)\oplus\hat{w}_Y^\perp$ pairs structural luminance preservation with target-directed transport through \textbf{\methoddec}, supporting real-time feed-forward (\textbf{\methodfast}), high-content half-NFE hybrid (\textbf{\methodhc}), and generative exemplar flow (\textbf{\methodflow}) regimes.

\textbf{Contributions.}
(1)~\emph{A Scaling Paradox in Latent Style Transfer:} Through large-scale controlled experiments and theoretical analysis (Prop.~\ref{prop:sourcelock}), we reveal \emph{Source-Style Lock}—showing that expanding self-paired flow capacity or decoder reconstruction fidelity can reinforce source-domain bias rather than improve cross-style transfer.
(2)~\emph{\method: Structure-Invariant, Style-Directed Latent Transport:} We introduce Quotient--Fiber disentanglement (Prop.~\ref{prop:stiefel}--\ref{prop:antisym}) and target-directed latent transport with spectral-bottleneck decoding (\textbf{\methoddec}), blocking source-texture backflow while preserving semantic geometry.
(3)~\emph{A New Content--Style--Compute Frontier:} We establish competitive cross-movement stylization across strong-style and high-content regimes at orders-of-magnitude lower compute ($\fastMs$ cached / $\fastBatchEtoEMs\text{--}\hcBatchMs$ batched RGB$\to$RGB; $\flowFps\text{--}\fastConsumerFps$ under $<3.9\,\text{GB}$ VRAM on a single consumer RTX~3060), verified by paired cluster bootstrap CIs and counterfactual controls.

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Conventional Lightweight Style Transfer.}
Classical NST evolved from iterative Gram matching~\cite{gatys2016image,simonyan2014vgg} to feed-forward moment alignment (AdaIN~\cite{huang2017arbitrary}, WCT~\cite{li2017universal}), pattern attention/transformers (StyTr$^2$~\cite{deng2022stytr2}, AesPA-Net~\cite{hong2023aespa}, AesFA~\cite{kwon2024aesfa}), unpaired contrastive translation (CUT~\cite{park2020cut}), histogram matching (Hist2Style~\cite{afifi2021histogan}), and state-space models (SaMam~\cite{liu2024samam}, SaMST~\cite{liu2024samst}). While lightweight ($0.2\text{--}3.0\,\text{s/img}$), global moment transforms corrupt boundaries ($\mathrm{DINO\text{-}C}<0.23$), whereas contrastive/Mamba translators preserve content by under-stylizing ($<12\%$ CSD Top-1).

\textbf{Diffusion-Heavy Editors, Flows, and Latent Tokenizers.}
Pretrained diffusion stylizers~\cite{meng2021sdedit,zhang2023inst,ye2023ipadapter,hertz2024style,chung2024style,gao2024styleshot,he2026stylegallery} achieve rich textures but incur massive compute: StyleShot ($2.4\text{B}$, $24.5\,\text{s/img}$) overwrites scene layout ($\mathrm{DINO\text{-}C}=0.3569$), while StyleGallery~\cite{he2026stylegallery} ($950\text{M}$, $30.4\,\text{s/img}$) requires 50-step DDIM inversion, feature clustering, and per-image attention optimization. Latent bridges~\cite{lipman2022flow,liu2022rectified,chadebec2025lbm,kim2024latentsb} and spatial adapters~\cite{zhang2023controlnet,park2019spade} reduce step counts, yet self-paired $64\times 64$ conditioning locks onto source strokes. Unlike reconstruction-oriented VAE compression~\cite{sadat2024litevae,lin2023favae,molchanov2019importance}, \textbf{\methoddec} uses structured pruning as a spectral bottleneck regularizer that suppresses source-texture retraction while accelerating decoding. We evaluate all paradigms under CSD~\cite{somepalli2024csd}, DINOv2~\cite{oquab2023dinov2}, CLIP~\cite{radford2021clip}, LPIPS~\cite{zhang2018unreasonable}, validity corridors~\cite{ioannou2024evaluation}, and counterfactual gates.

\section{Theory and Methodology}
\label{sec:method}

Given an unlabeled source image $x_s \in [0,1]^{3\times H\times W}$ with latent $z_s = \mathcal{E}(x_s) \in \R^{4\times h\times w}$ ($h=H/8, w=W/8$) and target specification $\tau$ (style class $y_t$ or exemplar $x_{\mathrm{ref}}$), \textbf{\method} synthesizes $\hat{x}_{s\to\tau} = \mathcal{D}_\star(\mathcal{T}_\tau(z_s, \Pi_\perp(x_s), \Pi_\parallel(x_s)))$ by explicitly blocking source-style backflow across three stages (Fig.~\ref{fig:arch}).

\subsection{Posterior Source-Lock in Self-Paired Flows}
\label{sec:sourcelock}

In self-paired flow matching~\cite{lipman2022flow}, velocity network $v_\theta(z_\tau,\tau,y)$ is trained on $z_\tau = (1-\tau)\epsilon + \tau z$. At inference, SDEdit~\cite{meng2021sdedit} initializes at $z_{\tau_0} = (1-\tau_0)\epsilon + \tau_0 z_s$ ($z_s\sim\Prob_{y_s}$) under target $y_t \neq y_s$.

\begin{proposition}[Posterior Source-Lock]
\label{prop:sourcelock}
With label dropout $p_{\mathrm{drop}}\in(0,1)$, the Bayes-optimal velocity at $(z_{\tau_0},\tau_0,y_t)$ is $\frac{\hat{z}_{\tau_0}(y_t)-z_{\tau_0}}{1-\tau_0}$, with posterior expectation under $\tilde{\Prob}_{y_t}=(1-\eta)\Prob_{y_t}+\eta\sum_k\pi_k\Prob_k$:
\begin{equation}
\hat{z}_{\tau_0}(y_t) = \frac{\int_{\mathcal{Z}} z \, \exp\!\Big(-\frac{\norm{z_{\tau_0} - \tau_0 z}_2^2}{2(1-\tau_0)^2}\Big) d\tilde{\Prob}_{y_t}(z)}{\int_{\mathcal{Z}} \exp\!\Big(-\frac{\norm{z_{\tau_0} - \tau_0 z}_2^2}{2(1-\tau_0)^2}\Big) d\tilde{\Prob}_{y_t}(z)}.
\label{eq:posterior-lock}
\end{equation}
For $\Delta_{s\to t}(z_s)=\mathrm{dist}(z_s,\mathrm{supp}(\Prob_{y_t}))^2$, whenever $\frac{\tau_0^2\Delta_{s\to t}(z_s)}{2(1-\tau_0)^2} \gg \log\frac{1-\eta}{\eta\pi_{y_s}}$, the Gaussian kernel concentrates exponentially on $z_s\in\mathrm{supp}(\Prob_{y_s})$, giving $\norm{\hat{z}_{\tau_0}(y_t)-z_s}_2=\mathcal{O}(1-\tau_0)$ regardless of $y_t$.
\end{proposition}
Expanding capacity ($0.13\text{M}\!\to\!34.2\text{M}$) sharpens local score curvature around $z_{\tau_0}$, worsening source-lock ($10.67\%\!\to\!6.67\%$), while $\tau_0=0$ self-paired $64\times 64$ edge conditioning still leaks 2D stroke cadence ($18.67\%$ ceiling).

\subsection{Operator I: Orthogonal Quotient--Fiber Duality $(\Pi_\perp, \Pi_\parallel)$}
\label{sec:quotient}

To prevent source texture leakage from entering the structural condition, we decompose $x$ under $\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{style}} = \ker(w_Y^\top) \times (\R^D\rtimes\R_+^D)$ into invariant quotient and complementary fiber coordinates:

\textbf{1) Invariant Structural Quotient $\Pi_\perp(x)=(C_{64},Q_k)$:}
From grayscale luminance $Y(x)=w_Y^\top x$ downsampled to $Y_{64}$ with local residual $\Delta_k Y=Y_{64}-\mathcal{A}_k(Y_{64})$ ($k\in\{5,13\}$), gradient-safe Local Contrast Normalization is
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{LCN}_k(Y_{64}) = \tfrac{1}{3}\mathrm{clip}\!\left(\frac{\Delta_k Y}{\sqrt{\max(\mathcal{A}_k(\Delta_k Y^2),\delta_0)} + \varepsilon_k},-3,3\right),
\label{eq:lcn}
\end{equation}
($\delta_0=10^{-8}$), forming $C_{64}(x)\in[-1,1]^{4\times 64^2}$ with quantile Sobel and RMS Laplacian maps. For semantic layout, we extract DINOv2-small~\cite{oquab2023dinov2} patch tokens $P\in\R^{D\times N}$ ($D=384, N=256$) from grayscale $[Y,Y,Y]$. Standard per-channel diagonal whitening $\hat{P}_d = (P_d - \mu_{\mathrm{sp}}(P_d))/(\sigma_{\mathrm{sp}}(P_d) + \varepsilon)$ only normalizes diagonal variances while leaving off-diagonal channel correlations intact, leaking $92.33\%$ 5-way style identity via 2nd-order Gram matrices.

\begin{proposition}[Stiefel Polar Quotient \& Zero 2nd-Order Gram Leakage]
\label{prop:stiefel}
Let $W_k \in \R^{D\times k}$ ($k=32 \ll N$) be an orthogonal semantic basis. For centered projection $Z = W_k^\top P(I_N - \frac{1}{N}\mathbf{1}\mathbf{1}^\top) \in \R^{k\times N}$ with compact SVD $Z = U \Sigma V^\top$ ($U\in O(k), V^\top V = I_k$), the Stiefel Polar Quotient
\begin{equation}
Q_k(x) = \sqrt{N}\, U V^\top \in \R^{k\times N}
\label{eq:stiefel-q}
\end{equation}
is the unique Frobenius-nearest matrix to $Z$ satisfying exact zero spatial mean ($Q_k(x)\mathbf{1} \equiv \mathbf{0}$) and exact identity sample Gram matrix ($\frac{1}{N} Q_k(x) Q_k(x)^\top \equiv I_k$).
\end{proposition}
By clamping all 2nd-order channel Gram statistics to $I_k$, $Q_{32}$ reduces 5-way Gram style probe accuracy on holdout paintings from $92.33\%$ to $\mathbf{20.00\%}$ (exact uniform-chance floor, numerical error $<2.3\times 10^{-4}$).

\textbf{2) Complementary Style Fiber $\Pi_\parallel(x)$ \& Target Style Chord:}
Conversely, $\Pi_\parallel(x)\in\R^{795}$ collects the discarded statistics—27-D Oklab color moments/quantiles/covariance plus 768-D raw RGB DINOv2 spatial $(\mu_{\mathrm{sp}},\sigma_{\mathrm{sp}})$. A 2-layer spherical encoder $\phi_\eta:\R^{795}\to\mathbb{S}^{127}$ trained with class barycenters $\{\bar{u}_y\}_{y=1}^K$ defines the \emph{source-label-free target chord}:
\begin{equation}
e(x_s\!\to\!\tau) = u_\tau - \phi_\eta(\Pi_\parallel(x_s)), \quad u_\tau \in \{\bar{u}_{y_t},\, \phi_\eta(\Pi_\parallel(x_{\mathrm{ref}}))\}.
\label{eq:universal-chord}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Quotient--Fiber Orthogonality \& Antisymmetry]
\label{prop:antisym}
For any chrominance shift $\Delta x\in\ker(w_Y^\top)$, $\Pi_\perp(x+\Delta x)\equiv\Pi_\perp(x)$ and $\mu_{\mathrm{sp}}(Q_k)\equiv\mathbf{0}$. Under self-reference ($x_{\mathrm{ref}}=x_s$), $e(x_s\to x_s)\equiv\mathbf{0}$ identically without style labels, and $e(\tau_1\!\to\!\tau_2)=-e(\tau_2\!\to\!\tau_1)$.
\end{proposition}

\subsection{Operator II: Controlled Latent Transport ($\mathcal{T}$)}
\label{sec:transport}

Regressing $Y_{64}\approx w_{\mathrm{luma}}^\top z + b_{\mathrm{luma}}$ on training latents yields unit luminance axis $\hat{w}_Y = w_{\mathrm{luma}}/\norm{w_{\mathrm{luma}}}_2 \in \R^4$, splitting $\R^4$ orthogonally into 1-D structural luminance $P_Y = \hat{w}_Y\hat{w}_Y^\top$ and 3-D chrominance $P_C = I_4 - P_Y$. Aligning $P_C z_0$ of wavelet anchor $z_0=\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{base}}(\mathcal{C}_1(z_s),y_t)$~\cite{daubechies1992ten} toward target chroma moments gives warm-start $z_0^\perp$ while keeping $P_Y z_0$ intact.

\textbf{1) \methodfast{} (Spatially Routed 1-Step Amortization):}
We distill $(z_0^\perp, z^\star)$ pairs into a $\fastStudentParams$ network $\Delta_\theta$ ($6$ width-$96$ dilated blocks) using Huber, Haar-HF, and differentiable S10-VGG+Oklab moment losses. A \emph{Semantic-Chord Router} interacts $Q_{32}$ with $e(x_s\!\to\!\tau)$ at $16\times 16$ to predict spatially routed modulation $(\Gamma_{64}, B_{64})$ under continuous strength $\lambda$:
\begin{equation}
\hat{z}_{s\to\tau}(\lambda) = z_0^\perp \!+\! \lambda\tanh^2(\norm{e}_2/\sigma_e)\Delta_\theta(z_0^\perp,P_Y z_s,C_{64},Q_{32};e).
\label{eq:prism-1step}
\end{equation}
When $e=\mathbf{0}$, $\tanh^2(\norm{e}_2/\sigma_e)=0$ enforces exact identity $\hat{z}\equiv z_0^\perp$.

\textbf{2) \methodflow{} \& \methodhc{} (Coarse-16 Flow \& Segmented CFG):}
For generative brushstroke synthesis, \textbf{\methodflow} filters training exemplars via intra-train $k$-NN-30 core density (eliminating 3D installation photo outliers) and restricts structural conditioning strictly to the $16\times 16$ bottleneck ($\mathrm{LCN}_{16}(Y)\oplus Q_{32}$ or whitened $P_{16}$) of a $22.0\text{M}$ U-Net, preventing $64\times 64$ source-edge locking. Under matched closed-set training (Table~\ref{tab:ablation}), replacing 384-ch $P_{16}$ with 32-ch $Q_{32}$ improves Top-1 ($55.67\%$ vs.\ $53.83\%$) and DINO-S ($0.5516$ vs.\ $0.5429$) at $12\times$ fewer condition channels. Controlled ablations further establish: (i) $30\%$ content dropout improves target recognition without training-time anchor bridges; (ii) content-conditioned 2-branch CFG (`cs\_j`: $v_{0c} + w_j(v_{sc}-v_{0c})$) halves flow evaluations vs.\ 4-branch `full`, while \textbf{segmented 6-step `e2full`} (applying `full` only on the first 2 of 6 RK2 steps before switching to `cs\_j`) preserves dense-to-sparse layout decluttering ($80.0\%$ \emph{Imp$\to$Min}) at $\flowSixCachedMs$ cached ($\flowSixQMs$ for $Q_{32}$ `cs\_j`). Combining the 1-step $z_0$ anchor (`B4`, $218\text{K}$) with half-NFE `cs\_j` flow and \textbf{\methodfast} linear mixing $(1-\gamma)\hat{z}_{\text{P1}} + \gamma\hat{z}_{\text{flow}}$ yields \textbf{\methodhc} ($\mathrm{DINO\text{-}C}\in[0.729,0.775]$, $\mathrm{LPIPS}\le 0.498$).

\subsection{Operator III: Spectral-Bottleneck Decoding ($\mathcal{D}_\star$)}
\label{sec:s10}

Partitioning the $49.49\text{M}$ SD-VAE decoder into four coupled channel streams, Taylor importance pruning~\cite{molchanov2019importance} reduces widths to $[64,128,192,256]$, yielding \textbf{\methoddec} ($\decParams$, $-\decPrune$). Beyond $\decSpeedup$ faster decoding and $\decGain$ higher cross-style Top-1 ($p<0.01$), its $4.7\times$ smaller memory footprint enables fast BF16 pixel-space gradient supervision during training.

\subsection{Redefining the Content--Style--Compute Frontier}

Table~\ref{tab:main}, Fig.~\ref{fig:page1}, and Fig.~\ref{fig:qual} demonstrate how \textbf{\method} bridges the gap between lightweight feed-forward nets and heavy diffusion:

\textbf{1) Real-Time Single-Step Dominance (\textbf{\methodfast}):}
Executing in $\fastMs$ protocol-cached ($\fastFps$; $\fastBatchEtoEMs$ max-batch / $\fastEtoEMs$ single-image full RGB$\to$RGB, $\fastConsumerFps$ on RTX~3060) with zero source labels, \textbf{\methodfast} ($\fastTopOne$ Top-1) simultaneously outperforms commercial Seedream~4.5 ($37.30\%$) and transformer-based StyTr$^2$ ($39.50\%$) across all five style, content, and distortion metrics ($+7.03\text{pp}/+4.83\text{pp}$ Top-1, $+0.0546/+0.0357$ DINO-S, $+0.0097/+0.0417$ DINO-C, and $-0.0145/-0.2329$ LPIPS), while achieving $96.7\%$ blind Same150 identity preservation.

\textbf{2) High-Content Pareto Breakthrough (\textbf{\methodhc}):}
In the high-content fidelity regime ($\mathrm{DINO\text{-}C}\in[0.729,0.775]$), combining the $218\text{K}$ `B4` structural anchor with half-NFE `cs\_j` flow (`D6H`) and \textbf{\methodfast} linear mixing pushes out the 3D $(\mathrm{DINO\text{-}C},\mathrm{DINO\text{-}S},\mathrm{LPIPS})$ Pareto frontier (Fig.~\ref{fig:page1}a). At $\gamma=0.55$, \textbf{\methodhc} ($\hcDinoC$ DINO-C, $\hcDinoS$ DINO-S, $\hcLpips$ LPIPS, $\hcTopOne$ Top-1) strictly dominates \textbf{\methodfast} ($\lambda=0.75$) on all four metrics under 150-cluster paired bootstrap CI ($\Delta\mathrm{DINO\text{-}S}=+0.0193\,[+0.0154,+0.0232]$, $\Delta\mathrm{LPIPS}=-0.0065\,[-0.0113,-0.0020]$, $\Delta\mathrm{Top\text{-}1}=+4.83\text{pp}$). At $\gamma=0.70$ ($\hcSeventyDinoC$ DINO-C, $\hcSeventyDinoS$ DINO-S, $\hcSeventyLpips$ LPIPS), it improves over T4-anchored Hybrid ($\gamma=0.25$) by $+0.0095$ DINO-C, $+0.0188$ DINO-S, and $-0.0189$ LPIPS (trading $-4.17\text{pp}$ CSD Top-1 vs.\ T4's sharper classifier margin), while single-model `B4+cs\_j+D6H` ($\gamma=1.0$) reaches $0.7286$ DINO-C and $0.4870$ DINO-S, breaking the $0.7055$ content saturation ceiling of T4-anchored single-model flows.

In the closed 5-style amortized regime, \textbf{\methodflow} reaches \textbf{\flowBalTopOne} Top-1, \textbf{\flowBalDinoS} DINO-S, and \textbf{\flowBalAff} Affinity (up to $76.83\%$ Top-1 in max-style mode), while its 6-step `e2full` mode achieves $58.00\%$ Top-1 and $0.6283$ DINO-C at $\flowSixCachedMs$ cached ($\flowSixEtoEMs$ E2E; and 6-step $Q_{32}$ `cs\_j` reaches $57.00\%$ / $0.6392$ Aff at $\flowSixQMs$ cached). \section{System Analysis and Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\textbf{End-to-End Stage Breakdown \& Consumer GPU Deployment.}
To clarify why ``NFE'' alone does not determine real-world responsiveness, we profile the full $512^2$ RGB$\to$RGB pipeline ($T_{\mathrm{total}} = T_{\mathrm{enc}} + T_{\mathrm{feat}} + T_{\mathrm{anc}} + T_{\mathrm{flow}} + T_{\mathrm{dec}}$) on a single 12\,GB RTX~3060 (`batch=1` / `batch=16`). At `b=16`, A0 VAE encoding takes $71.4\,\text{ms}$, DINOv2 extraction $57.9\,\text{ms}$, and S10 decoding $74.2\,\text{ms}$ (vs.\ $78.8 / 246.3 / 51.5\,\text{ms}$ at `b=1`). For \textbf{\methodfast}, the 1-step network takes only $\mathbf{5.8\,\text{ms}}$ (`b=1`) and $\mathbf{8.0\,\text{ms}}$ (`b=16`), yielding $\fastMs$ cached ($\fastFps$) and $\fastBatchEtoEMs$ max-batch full RGB$\to$RGB ($\fastConsumerFps$, vs.\ $\fastEtoEMs$ at `b=1`). For 6-step \textbf{\methodflow} (`b=16`), pure 2-branch `cs\_j` takes $98.5\,\text{ms}$ flow ($\flowSixQMs$ cached / $\flowSixQEtoEMs$ E2E with T4 anchor), while segmented `e2full` (2 `full` + 4 `cs\_j` steps) takes $130.7\,\text{ms}$ flow ($\flowSixCachedMs$ cached / $\flowSixEtoEMs$ E2E) vs.\ $520.0\,\text{ms}$ flow for 16-step `full` ($\flowEtoEMs$ E2E, $\flowFps$)—all within $3.7\text{--}3.9\,\text{GB}$ peak VRAM.

\textbf{Scope and Limitations.}
Our analysis shows that reconstruction capacity in tokenizers and self-paired flows is antagonistic to cross-domain editing unless source Gram statistics are projected out ($\Pi_\perp$). A clear boundary is that our headline 5-style models amortize a fixed training style repertoire; zero-label 1-shot exemplar generalization via chord transfer trades $-7.7\text{pp}$ Top-1 or lower DINO-C ($0.397\text{--}0.498$), and extreme layout-stripping routes (\emph{Imp$\to$Min}) require early 4-branch `e2full` CFG rather than pure `cs\_j`.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We showed that scaling self-paired latent flows ($260\times$) and using unpruned reconstruction decoders exacerbate \emph{Source-Style Lock}. By factoring style transfer into Stiefel Quotient--Fiber projections $(\Pi_\perp,\Pi_\parallel)$, target-directed transport $\mathcal{T}$, and a $\decPrune$-pruned spectral-bottleneck decoder $\mathcal{D}_\star$, \textbf{\method} eliminates 2nd-order Gram leakage ($92.3\%\!\to\!20.0\%$) and establishes a new Content--Style--Compute frontier across real-time ($\fastMs$ cached / $\fastBatchEtoEMs$ RGB$\to$RGB), high-content ($\hcDinoC$ DINO-C, $\hcLpips$ LPIPS, $\hcBatchMs$), and 6-step / 16-step generative ($\flowBalTopOne$ Top-1, $\flowBalDinoS$ DINO-S) regimes under $3.9\,\text{GB}$ on RTX~3060.
