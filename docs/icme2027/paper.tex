\begin{abstract}
Operating in the compressed latent space of a frozen variational autoencoder promises millisecond-scale artistic style transfer, yet existing editors split into two extremes: multi-billion-parameter diffusion models achieve strong stylization only by distorting scene geometry over seconds of denoising, whereas compact translators stall near identity or suffer perceptual artifacts. Through a controlled 250-run audit on 750 cross- and same-style pairs, we trace this barrier to \emph{Source-Style Lock}: self-paired trajectories, isotropic latent constraints, and over-parameterized reconstruction decoders preserve nuisance source-domain information that pulls edited latents back toward the input style. Strikingly, scaling a self-paired latent flow network by $260\times$ ($0.13\text{M}\!\to\!34.2\text{M}$) boosts unconditional generation from $20.0\%$ to $49.3\%$, yet monotonically \emph{degrades} cross-style SDEdit from $10.67\%$ to $6.67\%$ ($59\%$ source-style lock).

We propose \textbf{\method} (\emph{Projected Invariant Style Mapping}), a modular three-operator factorization $\mathcal{D}_\star \circ \mathcal{T}_{s\to t} \circ (\Pi_\perp,\Pi_\parallel)$ built on \emph{Orthogonal Quotient--Fiber Duality}. First, complementary projections decompose any input $x_s$ into a chrominance- and moment-free structural quotient $\Pi_\perp(x_s)$ ($18.3\%$ 5-way style leakage vs.\ $20\%$ uniform chance; $0.994$ layout alignment) and its complementary style fiber $\Pi_\parallel(x_s)$ ($97.3\%$ holdout style accuracy), enabling a \emph{source-label-free universal chord} $e(x_s\!\to\!\tau)=u_\tau-\phi(\Pi_\parallel(x_s))$ for both class prototypes and 1-shot exemplars. Second, splitting latent $\R^4$ into a 1-D structural luminance axis and 3-D chrominance subspace supports three Pareto-optimal regimes: a $\fastParams$ spatially routed single-step network (\textbf{\methodfast}, $\fastMs$), a 2-step S10-VGG/Patch-SWD solver (\textbf{\methodpro}, $\proTopOne\text{--}\proPlusTopOne$ Top-1), and a coarse-$16\times 16$ bottleneck flow anchored on intra-train $k$-NN core sub-modes (\textbf{\methodflow}, $\flowMs$). Third, $\decPrune$ structured channel pruning of the SD-VAE decoder (\textbf{\methoddec}, $\decParams$) acts as a spectral bottleneck regularizer that accelerates decoding by $\decSpeedup$ while \emph{increasing} cross-style accuracy by $\decGain$ ($p<0.01$). On 750 WikiArt tasks, single-step \textbf{\methodfast} ($\fastFps$) reaches $\fastTopOne\text{--}\fastStrongTopOne$ CSD Top-1 ($\shotTopOne$ 1-shot; $\fastWrongTopOne$ wrong-ID), unconditionally surpassing Seedream~4.5 ($37.30\%$) and StyTr$^2$ ($39.50\%$), while \textbf{\methodflow} reaches $\flowDinoS$ DINO-S, $\flowAff$ Style Affinity, and $\flowTopOne$ Top-1—surpassing diffusion cross-attention StyleGallery ($0.5604$ DINO-S, $0.6432$ Affinity, $52.50\%$ Top-1) at $226\times$ higher speed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Compressing images into a frozen variational autoencoder (VAE)~\cite{rombach2022high} reduces spatial resolution by $8\times$, suggesting that conditional style transfer—rendering an unlabeled input $x_s$ in target style $\tau$ while preserving scene geometry—should be solvable in millisecond-scale latent passes. Yet existing systems remain polarized (Fig.~\ref{fig:page1}). Diffusion editors~\cite{gao2024styleshot,chung2024style,hertz2024style,he2026stylegallery} achieve strong brushstroke similarity only by running iterative latent optimization or gigabyte U-Nets over $5\text{--}30\,\text{s/img}$, whereas compact transformer, contrastive, and Mamba translators~\cite{deng2022stytr2,park2020cut,liu2024samam} either distort contours or stay near identity.

Why do scaling and distillation stall in reconstruction-trained latent spaces? Across $250+$ controlled runs on a unified 750-task benchmark, we trace the failure to \textbf{Reconstruction--Transport Antagonism}: trajectories, latent metrics, and decoders optimized for \emph{self-reconstruction} preserve source-domain mutual information $I(\cdot\,;Y_s\mid C)$, creating \textbf{Source-Style Lock} across three interlocking paradoxes (Fig.~\ref{fig:paradoxes}):

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Trajectory Lock ($260\times$ Scaling Paradox, Fig.~\ref{fig:paradoxes}a):} Scaling a self-paired rectified flow~\cite{lipman2022flow,meng2021sdedit} by $260\times$ ($0.13\text{M}\!\to\!34.2\text{M}$) lifts pure noise generation ($\tau_0=0$) Top-1 from $20.0\%$ to $49.3\%$, yet monotonically \emph{lowers} SDEdit transfer ($\tau_0=0.7$) from $10.67\%$ to $\mathbf{6.67\%}$. Even $\tau_0=0$ pure-noise flows conditioned on chrominance-free edges stall at $18.67\%$ because self-paired training ($x_s\mapsto x_s$) inverts 2D stroke cadence back into $y_s$.
\item \textbf{Decoder Retraction \& Isotropic Palette Freeze (Fig.~\ref{fig:paradoxes}b):} Full $49.5\text{M}$ SD-VAE synthesis filters memorize natural/source priors; pruning $\decPrune$ of channels (\textbf{\methoddec}, $\decParams$) yields $\decSpeedup$ speedup and $\mathbf{\decGain}$ higher Top-1 ($p<0.01$). Simultaneously, standard GroupNorm latent critics normalize out palette moments while isotropic Euclidean trust balls penalize 3-D latent chrominance shifts as ``content loss,'' starving woodblock styles (Ukiyo-e $<1\%$).
\item \textbf{Evaluation Mirage (Fig.~\ref{fig:paradoxes}c):} Across $143$ models, ArtFID~\cite{wright2022artfid} correlates negatively with style ($\rho=-0.16$), while unanchored post-hoc scalers score \emph{higher} under counterfactual \emph{wrong} target labels than true ones ($13.0\%$ vs.\ $12.3\%$).
\end{enumerate}

To break Source-Style Lock without test-time source labels, \textbf{\method} (\emph{Projected Invariant Style Mapping}) introduces \textbf{Orthogonal Quotient--Fiber Duality} $(\Pi_\perp,\Pi_\parallel)$. While $\Pi_\perp(x_s)$ projects onto chrominance-free contrast and zero-mean spherical DINOv2 layout ($18.3\%$ style probe), its exact complement $\Pi_\parallel(x_s)$ collects the discarded color quantiles and DINO spatial moments ($97.3\%$ style accuracy) to form a \emph{universal source-free chord} $e(x_s\!\to\!\tau)=u_\tau-\phi(\Pi_\parallel(x_s))$. Splitting $\R^4=\mathrm{span}(\hat{w}_Y)\oplus\hat{w}_Y^\perp$ pairs tight luminance-contrast preservation with wide chrominance freedom under an un-normalized VGG+Oklab teacher through \textbf{\methoddec} (\textbf{\methodpro}), which distills into a $\fastParams$ spatially routed single-step network (\textbf{\methodfast}).

\textbf{Contributions.}
(1)~\emph{Source-Lock Theory \& Dual-Gate Audit:} We prove posterior source-lock in self-paired flows (Prop.~\ref{prop:sourcelock}) and establish Identity--Target validity plus counterfactual wrong-label causal gates.
(2)~\emph{Quotient--Fiber Duality \& Coarse-16 Core Transport:} Complementary $(\Pi_\perp,\Pi_\parallel)$ projections (Prop.~\ref{prop:quotient}) enable source-free chords (Prop.~\ref{prop:antisym}), anisotropic S10-VGG/SWD solving, intra-train $k$-NN core sub-mode flow, and spectral decoder pruning (\textbf{\methoddec}).
(3)~\emph{Unified Pareto Dominance:} Single-step \textbf{\methodfast} ($\fastMs$, $\fastTopOne\text{--}\fastStrongTopOne$ Top-1) beats Seedream~4.5 and StyTr$^2$ on all metrics; \textbf{\methodpro$^+$} reaches $\proPlusTopOne$ Top-1; and \textbf{\methodflow} ($\flowMs$) surpasses StyleGallery~\cite{he2026stylegallery} in DINO-S ($\flowDinoS$), Affinity ($\flowAff$), and Top-1 ($\flowTopOne$) at $226\times$ speedup.

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Neural Style Transfer.}
Classical NST progressed from Gram optimization~\cite{gatys2016image,simonyan2014vgg} to global moment transforms (AdaIN~\cite{huang2017arbitrary}, WCT~\cite{li2017universal}), attention/transformers (StyTr$^2$~\cite{deng2022stytr2}, AesPA-Net~\cite{hong2023aespa}, AesFA~\cite{kwon2024aesfa}), contrastive translation (CUT~\cite{park2020cut}), and Mamba models (SaMam~\cite{liu2024samam}, SaMST~\cite{liu2024samst}). Moment transforms often corrupt contours, while Mamba/contrastive models preserve content by under-stylizing ($<6\%$ CSD Top-1). \textbf{\method} routes semantic tokens in $64\times 64$ latents to reach $44.3\%\text{--}64.3\%$ Top-1.

\textbf{Latent Diffusion, Flows, and Tokenizers.}
Diffusion editors~\cite{meng2021sdedit,zhang2023inst,ye2023ipadapter,hertz2024style,chung2024style,gao2024styleshot,he2026stylegallery} either overwrite scene geometry or require multi-stage feature clustering and per-image attention optimization ($5\text{--}30\,\text{s/img}$); latent bridges~\cite{lipman2022flow,liu2022rectified,chadebec2025lbm,kim2024latentsb} and spatial adapters~\cite{zhang2023controlnet,park2019spade} shorten sampling, yet self-paired $64\times 64$ edge conditioning locks onto source brushstrokes. Unlike reconstruction-focused VAEs~\cite{sadat2024litevae,lin2023favae} or pure compression pruning~\cite{molchanov2019importance}, \textbf{\methoddec} leverages structured pruning both to suppress source-texture retraction and to enable fast differentiable pixel-space trajectory synthesis. For evaluation, we pair CSD~\cite{somepalli2024csd}, DINOv2~\cite{oquab2023dinov2}, CLIP~\cite{radford2021clip}, and LPIPS~\cite{zhang2018unreasonable} with validity~\cite{ioannou2024evaluation} and counterfactual causal gates.

\subsection{Posterior Source-Lock in Self-Paired Flows}
\label{sec:sourcelock}

In self-paired flow matching~\cite{lipman2022flow}, $v_\theta(z_\tau,\tau,y)$ is trained on $z_\tau = (1-\tau)\epsilon + \tau z$. At inference, SDEdit~\cite{meng2021sdedit} initializes at $z_{\tau_0} = (1-\tau_0)\epsilon + \tau_0 z_s$ ($z_s\sim\Prob_{y_s}$) under target $y_t \neq y_s$.

\begin{proposition}[Posterior Source-Lock]
\label{prop:sourcelock}
With label dropout $p_{\mathrm{drop}}\in(0,1)$, the Bayes-optimal velocity at $(z_{\tau_0},\tau_0,y_t)$ is $\frac{\hat{z}_{\tau_0}(y_t)-z_{\tau_0}}{1-\tau_0}$, with posterior expectation under $\tilde{\Prob}_{y_t}=(1-\eta)\Prob_{y_t}+\eta\sum_k\pi_k\Prob_k$:
\begin{equation}
\hat{z}_{\tau_0}(y_t) = \frac{\int_{\mathcal{Z}} z \, \exp\!\Big(-\frac{\norm{z_{\tau_0} - \tau_0 z}_2^2}{2(1-\tau_0)^2}\Big) d\tilde{\Prob}_{y_t}(z)}{\int_{\mathcal{Z}} \exp\!\Big(-\frac{\norm{z_{\tau_0} - \tau_0 z}_2^2}{2(1-\tau_0)^2}\Big) d\tilde{\Prob}_{y_t}(z)}.
\label{eq:posterior-lock}
\end{equation}
For $\Delta_{s\to t}(z_s)=\mathrm{dist}(z_s,\mathrm{supp}(\Prob_{y_t}))^2$, whenever $\frac{\tau_0^2\Delta_{s\to t}(z_s)}{2(1-\tau_0)^2} \gg \log\frac{1-\eta}{\eta\pi_{y_s}}$, the Gaussian kernel concentrates exponentially on $z_s\in\mathrm{supp}(\Prob_{y_s})$, giving $\norm{\hat{z}_{\tau_0}(y_t)-z_s}_2=\mathcal{O}(1-\tau_0)$ regardless of $y_t$.
\end{proposition}
Higher capacity ($0.13\text{M}\!\to\!34.2\text{M}$) sharpens local score curvature around $z_{\tau_0}$, worsening source-lock ($10.67\%\!\to\!6.67\%$), while $\tau_0=0$ self-paired edge conditioning still leaks 2D stroke cadence ($18.67\%$ ceiling).

\subsection{Operator I: Orthogonal Quotient--Fiber Duality $(\Pi_\perp, \Pi_\parallel)$}
\label{sec:quotient}

Global style acts via $\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{style}} = \ker(w_Y^\top) \times (\R^D\rtimes\R_+^D)$, combining RGB chrominance orthogonal to luminance $Y(x)=w_Y^\top x$ and spatial affine moments~\cite{huang2017arbitrary}. We decompose $x$ into orthogonal quotient and fiber coordinates:

\textbf{1) Invariant Structural Quotient $\Pi_\perp(x)=(C_{64},\tilde{P}_{16})$:}
Downsampling smoothed $Y(x)$ to $Y_{64}$ with $\Delta_k Y=Y_{64}-\mathcal{A}_k(Y_{64})$ ($k\in\{5,13\}$), gradient-safe Local Contrast Normalization is
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{LCN}_k(Y_{64}) = \tfrac{1}{3}\mathrm{clip}\!\left(\frac{\Delta_k Y}{\sqrt{\max(\mathcal{A}_k(\Delta_k Y^2),\delta_0)} + \varepsilon_k},-3,3\right),
\label{eq:lcn}
\end{equation}
with $\delta_0=10^{-8}$, forming $C_{64}(x)\in[-1,1]^{4\times 64^2}$ alongside quantile Sobel and RMS Laplacian maps. Extracting DINOv2-small~\cite{oquab2023dinov2} tokens $P\in\R^{384\times 16^2}$ from grayscale $[Y,Y,Y]$, we project onto the zero-mean spherical manifold $\mathcal{M}_0=\{Q: Q\mathbf{1}=\mathbf{0},\,\norm{Q_{:,u}}_2=\sqrt{D}\}$:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\hat{P}_d &= \frac{P_d - \mu_{\mathrm{sp}}(P_d)}{\sigma_{\mathrm{sp}}(P_d) + \varepsilon},\quad
\bar{P}_{:,u} = \sqrt{D}\frac{\hat{P}_{:,u}}{\norm{\hat{P}_{:,u}}_2},\\
\tilde{P}_{16} &= \bar{P} - \mu_{\mathrm{sp}}(\bar{P}).
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:whitened-dino}
\end{equation}

\textbf{2) Complementary Style Fiber $\Pi_\parallel(x)$ \& Universal Chord:}
Conversely, $\Pi_\parallel(x)\in\R^{795}$ collects the exact discarded statistics—perceptual Oklab color moments/quantiles/covariance ($27$-D) plus raw RGB DINOv2 spatial $(\mu_{\mathrm{sp}},\sigma_{\mathrm{sp}})$ ($768$-D). A 2-layer spherical encoder $\phi_\eta:\R^{795}\to\mathbb{S}^{127}$ trained with class barycenters $\{\bar{u}_y\}_{y=1}^K$ defines the \emph{source-label-free universal chord}:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
e(x_s\!\to\!\tau) &= u_\tau - \phi_\eta(\Pi_\parallel(x_s)),\\
u_\tau &= \bar{u}_{y_t}\;\text{(class)} \quad \text{or} \quad \phi_\eta(\Pi_\parallel(x_{\mathrm{ref}}))\;\text{(1-shot)}.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:universal-chord}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Quotient--Fiber Orthogonality \& Self-Identity]
\label{prop:quotient}
For any $\Delta x\in\ker(w_Y^\top)$, $\Pi_\perp(x+\Delta x)\equiv\Pi_\perp(x)$ and $\mu_{\mathrm{sp}}(\tilde{P}_{16})\equiv\mathbf{0}$. Moreover, under 1-shot self-reference ($x_{\mathrm{ref}}=x_s$), $e(x_s\to x_s)\equiv\mathbf{0}$ identically without style labels.
\end{proposition}

\subsection{Operator II: Anisotropic Luma--Chroma Chord Transport}
\label{sec:transport}

Least-squares regression $Y_{64}\approx w_{\mathrm{luma}}^\top z + b_{\mathrm{luma}}$ on training latents yields unit luminance axis $\hat{w}_Y = w_{\mathrm{luma}}/\norm{w_{\mathrm{luma}}}_2 \in \R^4$, splitting $\R^4$ orthogonally into 1-D structural luminance $P_Y = \hat{w}_Y\hat{w}_Y^\top$ and 3-D chrominance $P_C = I_4 - P_Y$. Aligning $P_C z_0$ of wavelet base anchor $z_0=\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{base}}(\mathcal{C}_1(z_s),y_t)$~\cite{daubechies1992ten} toward target chroma moments gives warm-start $z_0^\perp$ with zero change to $P_Y z_0$.

\textbf{1) \methodpro{} \& \methodflow{} (Anisotropic \& Coarse-16 Core Transport):}
GroupNorm layers inside latent critics normalize out palette moments. Differentiating through frozen BF16 \textbf{\methoddec} into un-normalized VGG-16~\cite{simonyan2014vgg} multi-scale $(\mu,\sigma)$, low-rank Gram matrices, and Oklab moments with balanced head $F_\psi$, \textbf{\methodpro} ascends calibrated margin $\tilde{m}_{F_\psi}(\mathcal{D}_\star(z),y_t) - \gamma\sum_{k\neq y_t}\hat{p}_{s,k}\tilde{f}_{\psi,k}(\mathcal{D}_\star(z))$ inside an anisotropic trust region ($\norm{P_Y^{\mathrm{edge}}\Delta z}_{\mathrm{RMS}}\le\rho_Y \ll \rho_C$); adding multi-scale ConvNeXt/RGB sliced Wasserstein refinement toward scene-matched exemplars yields \textbf{\methodpro$^+$}. To overcome hypersphere centroid collapse ($\max_r\cos(\hat{x},x_r)$ shrinks toward single-mean targets) and $64\times 64$ brushstroke tracing, \textbf{\methodflow} filters noisy boundary outliers via intra-training $k$-NN-30 core density, injects $[\mathrm{LCN}_3(Y_{16}),\tilde{P}_{16}]$ \emph{solely at the $16\times 16$ bottleneck} with post-GroupNorm continuous scale, and leaves $32^2/64^2$ blocks purely style-modulated to synthesize authentic brushwork along a split-horizon bridge from \textbf{\methodpro}.

\textbf{2) \methodfast{} (Spatially Routed Single-Step Amortization):}
We distill balanced $(z_0^\perp, z^\star)$ pairs into a $\fastStudentParams$ network $\Delta_\theta$ ($6$ width-$96$ dilated $[1,1,2,2,4,1]$ blocks, $53\times 53$ latent receptive field) using Huber, Haar-HF, and S10-VGG moment losses. Rather than spatially uniform FiLM, a \emph{Semantic-Chord Router} interacts $\tilde{P}_{16}(u)$ with $e=e(x_s\!\to\!\tau)$ at $16\times 16$ to predict spatially heterogeneous modulation $(\Gamma_{64}, B_{64})$, gated by continuous strength $\lambda$:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\hat{z}_{s\to\tau}(\lambda) &= z_0^\perp + \lambda\,g_{\mathrm{id}}(e)\cdot\Delta_\theta\big(z_0^\perp, P_Y z_s, C_{64}, \tilde{P}_{16};\, e\big),\\
g_{\mathrm{id}}(e) &= \tanh^2\!\big(\norm{e}_2 / \sigma_e\big).
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:prism-1step}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Continuous Zero-Chord Identity]
\label{prop:antisym}
Whenever $e=\mathbf{0}$ (e.g., $x_{\mathrm{ref}}=x_s$), $\tanh^2(\norm{e}_2/\sigma_e)=0$ enforces exact $\Delta\equiv\mathbf{0}$ for all $\theta$, while $e(\tau_1\!\to\!\tau_2)=-e(\tau_2\!\to\!\tau_1)$ preserves chord antisymmetry.
\end{proposition}

\subsection{Operator III: Spectral-Bottleneck Decoding ($\mathcal{D}_\star$)}
\label{sec:s10}

Partitioning the $49.49\text{M}$ SD-VAE decoder into four coupled channel streams, first-order Taylor importance~\cite{molchanov2019importance} $\mathcal{I}(g)=|\sum_{(c,i)\in g} W_{c,i}\partial\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{rec}}/\partial W_{c,i}|$ prunes widths to $[64,128,192,256]$ across five recovery stages, yielding \textbf{\methoddec} ($\decParams$, $-\decPrune$). Beyond $\decSpeedup$ faster inference ($15.6\,\text{ms}$ @ B8) and $\decGain$ higher cross-style Top-1, its $4.7\times$ smaller footprint enables fast BF16 pixel-gradient distillation in Operator~II.

\section{Broader Implications and Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\textbf{Implications for Generative Editing.}
\textbf{\method} establishes three broader principles:
(1)~\emph{Orthogonal Quotient--Fiber Duality} $(\Pi_\perp,\Pi_\parallel)$ turns the information discarded by structural invariance into a label-free continuous source coordinate ($99.3\%$ blind Same150; $\shotTopOne$ 1-shot);
(2)~\emph{Coarse-$16\times 16$ bottleneck conditioning and intra-train $k$-NN core prototypes} eliminate both $64\times 64$ source-edge tracing and hyperplane boundary outliers; and
(3)~\emph{Structured decoder pruning} serves simultaneously as a transfer-enhancing spectral regularizer and a fast differentiable bridge for trajectory distillation.

\textbf{Conclusion.}
By uniting $(\Pi_\perp,\Pi_\parallel)$ duality, anisotropic and coarse-16 core transport, and spectral-bottleneck decoding, \textbf{\method} achieves $\fastTopOne\text{--}\fastStrongTopOne$ single-step Cross600 Top-1 at $\fastFps$, $\proPlusTopOne$ in \textbf{\methodpro$^+$}, and $\flowDinoS$ DINO-S / $\flowAff$ Affinity in \textbf{\methodflow}—surpassing StyleGallery at $226\times$ speedup.
