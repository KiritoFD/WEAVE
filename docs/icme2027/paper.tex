\title{\method: Real-Time Quotient-Guided P/E-Core Transport for Full-Spectrum Style Transfer}

\author{\IEEEauthorblockN{Anonymous ICME Submission}
\IEEEauthorblockA{Paper ID: ****}}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
\emph{More capacity can preserve the wrong information. Effective style transfer requires selective information removal throughout the entire generative pipeline.} Across $300+$ controlled runs on a 750-task benchmark, we uncover three counter-intuitive pathologies where preserving raw representation capacity locked onto the source image actively impedes stylization: (1)~standard spatial feature tokens leak $91.33\%$ 5-way source style identity through 2nd-order Gram correlations; (2)~scaling a self-paired latent flow by $260\times$ ($0.13\text{M}\!\to\!34.2\text{M}$) improves unconditional generation ($20.0\%\!\to\!49.3\%$) yet degrades cross-style SDEdit ($10.67\%\!\to\!6.67\%$) due to Posterior Source-Lock; and (3)~a full $49.5\text{M}$ SD-VAE decoder pulls stylized latents back toward natural-image textures. We propose \textbf{\method} (\textbf{C}ontinuous \textbf{L}atent \textbf{E}diting and \textbf{A}bstraction \textbf{V}elocity \textbf{E}ngine), a real-time \textbf{Performance--Efficiency (P/E)-Core} latent transport framework that deploys targeted information filters across all three stages under a single continuous knob $s\in[0,1.50]$. First, in conditioning space, a $\cnxParams$ ConvNeXt-Tiny Stage-3 extractor ($0.985\,\text{ms/img}$) is projected onto a \textbf{Stiefel Polar Quotient} ($Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}}$), clamping sample Gram matrices to $I_{32}$ and stripping 5-way style predictability from $91.33\%$ to the $20.67\%$ chance floor while preserving pure spatial geometry. Second, in trajectory space, a Hybrid Director dispatches regimes across asymmetric cores: a 1-NFE \textbf{\methodecore} ($\ecoreParams$) retains gated $64\times 64$ contours for high-content editing ($s\in[0,0.75]$) with exact $s=0$ identity ($\max\lvert\hat{z}-z_s\rvert=0.0$) and emits $1\,\text{ms}$ structural drafts $z_{\mathrm{E}}$, while a 6-step DPM-2M \textbf{\methodpcore} ($\pcoreParams$) drops $64^2/32^2$ structural skips ($16\times 16$ bottleneck only) and trains on $15\%$ in-domain reversed real-painting pairs (\texttt{Pool C}) to break source-lock across $s\in[0.80,1.50]$. Third, in decoding space, a $\decPrune$-pruned spectral-bottleneck decoder (\textbf{\methoddec}, $\decParams$) removes natural-texture priors ($\decGain\text{--}\decClosedGain$ Top-1 at $\decSpeedup$ speed). On \textbf{Cross600}, \textbf{\method} spans $\sZeroFortyC \to \sOneFiftyC$ DINO-C and $\sZeroFortyTop \to \sOneFiftyTop$ CSD Top-1 at $\pcoreColdBatchFps\text{--}\ecoreColdBatchFps$ true cold-start RGB$\to$RGB ($\pcoreHotBatchFps\text{--}\ecoreHotBatchFps$ hot interactive)---$550\times\text{--}1{,}000\times$ faster than StyleGallery ($30.4\,\text{s/img}$) with zero DINOv2 backbone dependency for $100\%$ blind evaluation parity.
\end{abstract}

\label{sec:intro}

Interactive artistic authoring requires a single real-time engine that sweeps smoothly across the full style--content spectrum, from structure-faithful palette adaptation to bold, abstract brushstroke reorganization. Operating in the $1/8$-resolution latent space of a variational autoencoder (VAE)~\cite{rombach2022high} should in principle make full-spectrum control achievable within milliseconds. Yet existing approaches remain fractured into two incompatible regimes (Fig.~\ref{fig:page1}): billion-parameter zero-shot diffusion editors~\cite{gao2024styleshot,chung2024style,he2026stylegallery,seedream2025} require $5\text{--}30.4\,\text{s/img}$ of iterative inversion or regional optimization to overwrite source textures, whereas lightweight feed-forward networks~\cite{deng2022stytr2,hong2023aespa,park2020cut,liu2024samam,afifi2021histogan} either under-stylize to protect contours ($<40\%$ CSD Top-1) or collapse spatial geometry under global moment shifts.

Through $300+$ controlled runs on a 750-task benchmark, we show that this trade-off is not caused by insufficient model capacity, but by \emph{unfiltered source-style backflow}. Across the generative pipeline, excess capacity preserves three forms of source-image information that directly conflict with target stylization (see Fig.~\ref{fig:arch} for our three-stage filter architecture and Fig.~\ref{fig:paradoxes} in Sec.~\ref{sec:experiments} for empirical curves):

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Conditioning-Space Source-Style Leakage (Fig.~\ref{fig:paradoxes}a):} Standard spatial feature tokens retain strong off-diagonal channel correlations whose 2nd-order Gram matrices allow a linear probe to predict the source painting's artistic movement with $91.33\%$ accuracy. Feeding such tokens into a conditional generator forces it to reconstruct source brushwork.
\item \textbf{Trajectory-Space Posterior Source-Lock (The P/E-Core Necessity, Fig.~\ref{fig:paradoxes}b):} High-content editing ($s\le 0.75$) is an injective $1$-to-$1$ transport task that \emph{must preserve} $64\times 64$ micro-contours with zero drift at $s=0$. Conversely, strong painterly abstraction ($s\ge 0.80$) is a stochastic $1$-to-many synthesis task that \emph{must erase} source micro-edges to reorganize macro planes. No single network can simultaneously preserve and erase $64\times 64$ contours: scaling a single self-paired flow~\cite{lipman2022flow,meng2021sdedit} by $260\times$ ($0.13\text{M}\!\to\!34.2\text{M}$) sharpens local reconstruction basins around $z_s$, lifting unconditional generation ($\tau_0=0$) from $20.0\%$ to $49.3\%$ while degrading cross-style SDEdit ($\tau_0=0.7$) from $10.67\%$ to $\mathbf{6.67\%}$ ($59\%$ locked back to source style).
\item \textbf{Decoding-Space Texture Retraction (Fig.~\ref{fig:paradoxes}c):} Over-parameterized $49.5\text{M}$ SD-VAE synthesis filters memorize natural-image high-frequency priors that pull stylized latents back toward unedited appearance; pruning $\decPrune$ of decoder channels accelerates synthesis by $\decSpeedup$ while \emph{increasing} cross-style Top-1 by $\decGain\text{--}\decClosedGain$.
\end{enumerate}

We introduce \textbf{\method} (\textbf{C}ontinuous \textbf{L}atent \textbf{E}diting and \textbf{A}bstraction \textbf{V}elocity \textbf{E}ngine)---a name capturing its dual physical mechanism: \emph{cleaving away} unwanted source style across conditioning, trajectory, and decoding spaces while \emph{cleaving closely} to source geometry in high-content regimes (Fig.~\ref{fig:arch}). Our contributions align 1-to-1 with three selective information filters:
(1)~\emph{Conditioning Filter (Stiefel Polar Quotient $Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}}$ \& Sub-School Fiber):} Using a lightweight $\cnxParams$ \textbf{ConvNeXt-Tiny Stage-3} front-end ($0.985\,\text{ms/img}$ at \texttt{b=16}), Stiefel polar projection clamps sample Gram matrices exactly to $I_{32}$ (error $1.95\times 10^{-4}$), eliminating 2nd-order style leakage ($91.33\%\!\to\!20.67\%$ chance floor) while retaining $+0.0742$ DINO-C spatial geometry alongside a 417-D $K=8$ sub-school style bank.
(2)~\emph{Trajectory Filter (Performance-Hybrid P/E-Core Transport $\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{P/E}}$):} Rather than a mere hardware heuristic, our \textbf{P/E-Core} architecture performs trajectory-level selective information gating under a Single-Knob Hybrid Director. For $s\in[0,0.75]$, 1-NFE \textbf{\methodecore} ($\ecoreParams$) retains gated $64\times 64$ contours over an orthogonal luma--chroma split ($P_Y\oplus P_C$), guaranteeing exact $s=0$ identity ($\max\lvert\hat{z}-z_s\rvert=0.0$) and emitting $0.99\,\text{ms}$ pre-shifted drafts $z_{\mathrm{E}}$ for higher regimes. For $s\in[0.80,1.50]$, 6-step DPM-2M \textbf{\methodpcore} ($\pcoreParams$) removes $64^2/32^2$ structural skips to inject geometry strictly at its $16\times 16$ bottleneck and trains on $15\%$ in-domain reversed real-painting pairs (\texttt{Pool C}) to break Posterior Source-Lock.
(3)~\emph{Decoding Filter (\methoddec) \& Blind Systems Ledger:} Taylor-pruned \textbf{\methoddec} ($\decParams$, $-\decPrune$ channels) acts as a spectral bottleneck that blocks texture retraction ($\decGain\text{--}\decClosedGain$ Top-1). We disclose both true cold-start RGB$\to$RGB ($\pcoreColdBatchFps\text{--}\ecoreColdBatchFps$, $\coldTotalParams$) and hot interactive ($\pcoreHotBatchFps\text{--}\ecoreHotBatchFps$, $\hotTotalParams$) budgets and verify via paired backbone audits that our gains are backbone-independent. \emph{More capacity can preserve the wrong information; compactness in \textbf{\method} is a by-product of principled information removal, not a quality compromise.}

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Lightweight Feed-Forward Style Transfer.}
Classical neural style transfer evolved from Gram optimization~\cite{gatys2016image,simonyan2014vgg} to moment matching (AdaIN~\cite{huang2017arbitrary}, WCT~\cite{li2017universal}), attention/transformer modules (StyTr$^2$~\cite{deng2022stytr2}, AesPA-Net~\cite{hong2023aespa}, AesFA~\cite{kwon2024aesfa}), contrastive translation (CUT~\cite{park2020cut}), histogram alignment (Hist2Style~\cite{afifi2021histogan}), and state-space networks (SaMam~\cite{liu2024samam}, SaMST~\cite{liu2024samst}). From an information-filtering perspective, global moment transforms indiscriminately overwrite spatial geometry, whereas conservative translators retain unwhitened high-frequency source skips that lock onto source textures.

\textbf{Zero-Shot Diffusion Editors, Latent Bridges, and Decoder Pruning.}
Foundation and diffusion stylizers~\cite{meng2021sdedit,zhang2023inst,ye2023ipadapter,hertz2024style,chung2024style,gao2024styleshot,he2026stylegallery,seedream2025} rely on billion-parameter priors and $5\text{--}30.4\,\text{s/img}$ sampling---via attention injection (StyleID~\cite{chung2024style}, StyleShot~\cite{gao2024styleshot}), multimodal generation (Seedream~4.5~\cite{seedream2025}), or 150-step DINOv2-guided inversion and patch matching (StyleGallery~\cite{he2026stylegallery}). Latent bridges~\cite{lipman2022flow,liu2022rectified,chadebec2025lbm,kim2024latentsb} and spatial adapters~\cite{zhang2023controlnet,park2019spade} reduce step counts but suffer from Posterior Source-Lock under self-paired $64\times 64$ conditioning. Finally, whereas prior VAE pruning~\cite{sadat2024litevae,lin2023favae,molchanov2019importance} targets reconstruction speed, \textbf{\methoddec} leverages structured pruning to filter out source-retracting natural-image priors.

\section{Methodology: Three-Stage Information Filtering}
\label{sec:method}

Given an unlabeled source image $x_s \in [0,1]^{3\times H\times W}$ with VAE latent $z_s = \mathcal{E}(x_s) \in \R^{4\times 64\times 64}$ ($H=W=512$) and target movement $y_t \in \{1,\dots,5\}$ under single knob $s \in [0, 1.50]$, \textbf{\method} synthesizes $\hat{x}_{s\to y_t}(s) = \mathcal{D}_\star(\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{P/E}}(z_s, \Pi_\perp^{\mathrm{cnx}}(x_s), \Pi_\parallel^{\mathrm{cnx}}(x_s, y_t); s))$ through three sequential information filters (Fig.~\ref{fig:arch}).

\subsection{Conditioning-Space Filtering: Stiefel Polar Quotient and Sub-School Fiber}
\label{sec:quotient}

\textbf{1) Luminance Structure $C_{64}$ and Coarse $Y_{16}$:}
From ITU-R BT.601 grayscale luminance $Y(x)=w_Y^\top x$ downsampled to $Y_{64}$ with local residual $\Delta_k Y=Y_{64}-\mathcal{A}_k(Y_{64})$ ($k\in\{5,13\}$), gradient-safe Local Contrast Normalization is
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{LCN}_k(Y_{64}) = \tfrac{1}{3}\mathrm{clip}\!\left(\frac{\Delta_k Y}{\sqrt{\max(\mathcal{A}_k(\Delta_k Y^2),\delta_0)} + \varepsilon_k},-3,3\right),
\label{eq:lcn}
\end{equation}
($\delta_0=10^{-8}$), stacked with 95th-percentile Sobel and RMS Laplacian maps into $C_{64}(x)\in[-1,1]^{4\times 64\times 64}$ for \textbf{\methodecore}, and pooled to $16\times 16$ $\mathrm{LCN}_3(Y_{16})\in[-1,1]^{1\times 16\times 16}$ (32px blocks, zero micro-stroke edges) for \textbf{\methodpcore}.

\textbf{2) ConvNeXt-Stage3 Stiefel Polar Quotient $Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}}$:}
We truncate an ImageNet-pretrained ConvNeXt-Tiny backbone at Stage~3 (\texttt{features[:6]}, $\cnxParams$ parameters, $D=384$ at stride~$16$). Feeding $256\times 256$ grayscale $[Y,Y,Y]$ yields spatial tokens $P_{\mathrm{cnx}}\in\R^{384\times 256}$ in $\mathbf{0.985\,\text{ms/img}}$ (\texttt{b=16}), avoiding both $86\text{M}+$ ViT latency and evaluator homology. Standard diagonal channel whitening leaves off-diagonal correlations intact ($91.33\%$ 5-way Gram style leakage). To strip 2nd-order style correlations completely while retaining spatial layout, we project onto the Stiefel manifold:

\begin{proposition}[Stiefel Polar Quotient \& Zero 2nd-Order Gram Predictability]
\label{prop:stiefel}
Let $W_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}} \in \R^{384\times 32}$ be the principal semantic basis fit on training grayscale tokens. For spatially centered projection $Z = (W_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}})^\top P_{\mathrm{cnx}}(I_N - \frac{1}{N}\mathbf{1}\mathbf{1}^\top) \in \R^{32\times N}$ ($N=256$) with compact SVD $Z = U \Sigma V^\top$ ($U\in O(32), V^\top V = I_{32}$), the Stiefel Polar Quotient
\begin{equation}
Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}}(x) = \sqrt{N}\, U V^\top \in \R^{32\times 16\times 16}
\label{eq:stiefel-q}
\end{equation}
is the unique Frobenius-nearest tensor to $Z$ satisfying exact zero spatial mean ($Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}}\mathbf{1} \equiv \mathbf{0}$) and exact identity sample Gram matrix ($\frac{1}{N} Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}} (Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}})^\top \equiv I_{32}$).
\end{proposition}
On 300 holdout paintings, $Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}}$ achieves a maximum Gram identity numerical error of $1.95\times 10^{-4}$ and slashes 5-way Gram style probe accuracy from $91.33\%$ to $\mathbf{20.67\%}$ ($20.0\%$ uniform chance floor).

\textbf{3) 417-D $K=8$ Sub-School Style Bank and Scene Router:}
To supply clean target style attributes, we extract $f_{417}(x) = [\text{Oklab}_{27}(x) \;\|\; \text{Haar}_{6}(x) \;\|\; \mu_{\mathrm{RGB}}^{\mathrm{ConvNeXt\text{-}S3}}(x)] \in \R^{417}$, combining 27-D Oklab color moments/quantiles/covariances, 6-D 3-level Haar wavelet log-energy ratios, and the 384-D global spatial mean of RGB ConvNeXt-Stage3 features. Within each style's $k$-NN-30 core training manifold, spherical $k$-means partitions exemplars into $K=8$ sub-schools with centroids $\{u_{y_t,k}^{\mathrm{cnx}}\}_{k=1}^8 \subset \R^{417}$ and $4\times 4$-pooled grayscale scene descriptors $\{s_{y_t,k}^{\mathrm{cnx}}\}_{k=1}^8 \subset \R^{6144}$. At inference ($0.025\,\text{ms/img}$), source scene layout $s(x_s)=\mathrm{norm}(\mathrm{AvgPool}_{4\times 4}(P_{\mathrm{cnx}}(x_s)))$ routes to the compatible target sub-school $k^\star = \arg\max_k \langle s(x_s), s_{y_t,k}^{\mathrm{cnx}}\rangle$.

\subsection{Trajectory-Space Filtering: Posterior Source-Lock and P/E-Core Transport}
\label{sec:transport}

In standard self-paired flow matching~\cite{lipman2022flow}, velocity field $v_\theta(z_\tau,\tau,y)$ is trained on $z_\tau = (1-\tau)\epsilon + \tau z$ and evaluated via SDEdit~\cite{meng2021sdedit} from $z_{\tau_0} = (1-\tau_0)\epsilon + \tau_0 z_s$ ($z_s\sim\Prob_{y_s}$) under target $y_t \neq y_s$.

\begin{proposition}[Posterior Source-Lock]
\label{prop:sourcelock}
With label dropout $p_{\mathrm{drop}}\in(0,1)$, the Bayes-optimal velocity at $(z_{\tau_0},\tau_0,y_t)$ is $\frac{\hat{z}_{\tau_0}(y_t)-z_{\tau_0}}{1-\tau_0}$, where the posterior expectation under $\tilde{\Prob}_{y_t}=(1-\eta)\Prob_{y_t}+\eta\sum_k\pi_k\Prob_k$ is
\begin{equation}
\hat{z}_{\tau_0}(y_t) = \frac{\int_{\mathcal{Z}} z \, \exp\!\Big(-\frac{\norm{z_{\tau_0} - \tau_0 z}_2^2}{2(1-\tau_0)^2}\Big) d\tilde{\Prob}_{y_t}(z)}{\int_{\mathcal{Z}} \exp\!\Big(-\frac{\norm{z_{\tau_0} - \tau_0 z}_2^2}{2(1-\tau_0)^2}\Big) d\tilde{\Prob}_{y_t}(z)}.
\label{eq:posterior-lock}
\end{equation}
For $\Delta_{s\to t}(z_s)=\mathrm{dist}(z_s,\mathrm{supp}(\Prob_{y_t}))^2$, whenever $\frac{\tau_0^2\Delta_{s\to t}(z_s)}{2(1-\tau_0)^2} \gg \log\frac{1-\eta}{\eta\pi_{y_s}}$, the Gaussian kernel concentrates exponentially on $z_s\in\mathrm{supp}(\Prob_{y_s})$, yielding $\norm{\hat{z}_{\tau_0}(y_t)-z_s}_2=\mathcal{O}(1-\tau_0)$ regardless of $y_t$.
\end{proposition}
Prop.~\ref{prop:sourcelock} proves why a single self-paired flow cannot span the full spectrum: high $\tau_0$ locks the trajectory onto $z_s$, whereas lowering $\tau_0$ without a stylistically pre-shifted draft destroys fine content geometry. We therefore partition $s\in[0,1.50]$ across two asymmetric cores over an orthogonal luma--chroma split $P_Y = \hat{w}_Y\hat{w}_Y^\top$, $P_C = I_4 - P_Y$ (fit via $Y_{64}\approx w_{\mathrm{luma}}^\top z + b_{\mathrm{luma}}$):

\textbf{1) \methodecore{} ($\ecoreParams$, 1-NFE, $s \in [0, 0.75]$ --- Selective Contour Retention \& Draft Emission):}
In the high-content regime, \textbf{\methodecore} executes a single feed-forward pass ($\mathbf{0.986\,\text{ms/img}}$ network, $\ecoreColdBatchMs$ cold RGB$\to$RGB) using a gated hybrid U-Net conditioned on $(z_s, C_{64}, Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}}, y_t, \lambda)$ and class-level 417-D statistics:
\begin{equation}
z_{\mathrm{E}}(x_s, y_t; \lambda) \!=\! z_0^\perp(z_s, y_t; \lambda) + \lambda \Delta_{\theta_{\mathrm{E}}}\!\big(z_s, \gamma_{\mathrm{skip}}(\lambda) C_{64}, Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}}, y_t\big),
\label{eq:ecore}
\end{equation}
where $z_0^\perp(z_s,y_t;0)\equiv z_s$ and encoder skip gate $\gamma_{\mathrm{skip}}(\lambda)=\max(0, 1-0.8\lambda)$ gradually releases $64\times 64$ contours as $\lambda$ grows. At $\lambda=0$ ($s=0$), Eq.~\eqref{eq:ecore} guarantees \textbf{exact mathematical identity} $\hat{z}\equiv z_s$ (measured max latent drift $\mathbf{0.0}$). For $s > 0.75$, \textbf{\methodecore} emits a $0.99\,\text{ms}$ draft $z_{\mathrm{E}}(\lambda_{\mathrm{draft}}(s))$ that pre-shifts chrominance and coarse brushwork to move initialization out of the source attraction basin.

\textbf{2) \methodpcore{} ($\pcoreParams$, 6-Step DPM-2M Flow, $s \in [0.80, 1.50]$ --- Micro-Edge Removal \& Reversed Pairs):}
In the abstraction regime, \textbf{\methodpcore} initializes at $z_{\tau_0} = (1-\tau_0(s))\epsilon + \tau_0(s) z_{\mathrm{E}}(x_s, y_t; \lambda_{\mathrm{draft}}(s))$ (with $\tau_0(1.50)=0$) and integrates 6 steps of 2nd-order multistep DPM-Solver++ (\texttt{dpm2m}) with 4-branch decoupled classifier-free guidance:
\begin{equation}
v_{\mathrm{CFG}} = v_{00} + w_c(v_{0c}\!-\!v_{00}) + w_s(v_{sc}\!-\!v_{0c}) + w_{sp}(v_{s0}\!-\!v_{00}).
\label{eq:pcore-cfg}
\end{equation}
Crucially, \textbf{\methodpcore} \emph{removes all $64^2$ and $32^2$ structural skip connections}, injecting $(\mathrm{LCN}_3(Y_{16}), \gamma_{\mathrm{layout}}(s) Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}})$ \emph{exclusively at the $16\times 16$ bottleneck} while modulating $32^2/64^2$ synthesis blocks solely by $u_{y_t,k^\star}^{\mathrm{cnx}}$. To break Posterior Source-Lock during training ($3{,}500$ steps, $27.1\,\text{min}$ on one RTX~4090), each batch mixes $85\%$ $k$-NN-30 core paintings with $15\%$ curated \textbf{in-domain reversed real-painting pairs (\texttt{Pool C})}---pairing real ground-truth targets ($z_{\mathrm{real}}, y_t$) from under-represented movements (600 ER + 600 Uki) with de-styled structural tokens $(\mathrm{LCN}_3(Y_{16}), Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}})$ from synthetic cross-style sources---jointly supervised by high-$\tau$ pixel-space sliced Wasserstein distance (SWD) through \textbf{\methoddec}. A Single-Knob Hybrid Director maps $s\in[0,1.50]$ deterministically to \textbf{\methodecore} ($s\le 0.75$) or \textbf{\methodpcore} ($s\ge 0.80$).

\subsection{Decoding-Space Filtering: Spectral-Bottleneck Pruning (\methoddec)}
\label{sec:decoder}

Over-parameterized synthesis filters in the $49.49\text{M}$ SD-VAE decoder memorize natural-image micro-textures that retract stylized latents toward photographic appearance. We apply 4-stream Taylor importance pruning~\cite{molchanov2019importance} ($\mathcal{I}(g)=\lvert\sum W\,\partial\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{rec}}/\partial W\rvert$) to remove $\decPrune$ of decoder channels down to widths $[64,128,192,256]$ (\textbf{\methoddec}, $\decParams$), converting the decoder into a high-frequency spectral filter that blocks source-texture retraction ($\decGain\text{--}\decClosedGain$ Top-1) at $\decSpeedup$ speed.

\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Benchmark, Stratified Baselines, and Dual-Latency Protocol}

We evaluate at $512\times 512$ across five WikiArt movements~\cite{wright2022artfid}: \emph{Early Renaissance} (ER), \emph{Impressionism} (Imp), \emph{Minimalism} (Min), \emph{Rococo} (Roc), and \emph{Ukiyo-e} (Uki). Strictly disjoint from 4,400 training and 300 holdout paintings, 150 test sources $\times$ 5 targets form \textbf{750 tasks}: \textbf{Cross600} ($600$ cross-style pairs) and \textbf{Same150} ($150$ identity controls). Five frozen referee metrics run in isolated processes: \textbf{CSD-ViT-L}~\cite{somepalli2024csd} (5-way \emph{Top-1} and nearest-neighbor \emph{CSLS} $k=10$), \textbf{DINOv2-small}~\cite{oquab2023dinov2} (\emph{DINO-S} Max and Top-5 Mean; \emph{DINO-C} content cosine), \textbf{CLIP ViT-B/32}~\cite{radford2021clip} (\emph{CLIP-S}), and \textbf{LPIPS}~\cite{zhang2018unreasonable}. \subsection{Full-Spectrum Pareto Dominance and Representation-Space Affinity}

Table~\ref{tab:main}, Fig.~\ref{fig:page1}, and Fig.~\ref{fig:qual} establish three key findings:

\textbf{1) High-Content Dominance Over Tier~1 and Foundation Editors (\methodecore):}
Operating in $\ecoreColdBatchMs$ cold RGB$\to$RGB ($\ecoreColdBatchFps$ at \texttt{b=16}; $\ecoreHotBatchMs / \ecoreHotBatchFps$ hot interactive) with $98.0\%\text{--}98.7\%$ Same150 identity preservation, 1-NFE \textbf{\methodecore} strictly dominates all Tier~1 methods: at $s=0.40$ ($\sZeroFortyC$ DINO-C, $\sZeroFortyS$ DINO-S, $\sZeroFortyTop$ Top-1, $0.3503$ LPIPS) it beats CUT and SaMam on every metric; at $s=0.75$ ($\sZeroSeventyFiveC$ DINO-C, $\sZeroSeventyFiveS$ DINO-S, $\sZeroSeventyFiveTop$ Top-1, $0.4734$ LPIPS) it simultaneously outperforms StyTr$^2$ ($39.5\%$, $3.0\,\text{s}$), Seedream~4.5 ($37.3\%$, $20.0\,\text{s}$), and $15\text{B}$ Qwen-Image-2.1-Turbo ($42.2\%$, $16.1\,\text{s}$) across all five style and content metrics at $100\times\text{--}660\times$ higher speed. \textbf{2) Mid-Spectrum Calibration, Strong-Style Crossing, and StyleGallery Comparison (\methodpcore):}

\textbf{2) Backbone-Independence, Homology Offset, and Hubness Attribution (Table~\ref{tab:system}B):}
Comparing our ConvNeXt-Stage3 P/E-Core models against DINOv2-conditioned twins trained under identical steps and losses (Table~\ref{tab:system}B) proves two points: (i)~at $s=1.00$ and $s=1.25$, \emph{DINO-S} ($\Delta=+0.0023, -0.0015$), \emph{Style Affinity} ($\Delta=-0.0002$), and \emph{CSD Top-1} ($\Delta=-1.83\text{pp}, -1.67\text{pp}$) all have $95\%$ CIs spanning zero, confirming that \textbf{\method}'s quotient--fiber transport is \textbf{backbone-independent}; and (ii)~replacing DINOv2 with ConvNeXt-Stage3 shifts \emph{DINO-C} by a uniform $-0.023\text{ to }-0.032$ even when independent perceptual distortion \emph{LPIPS} is tied or slightly improved ($\Delta\mathrm{LPIPS}=-0.0038$ at $s=0.90\text{ vs }0.85$), quantifying for the first time a $\sim 0.025$ \emph{DINO-C} scale offset induced by generator--evaluator ViT homology. At the extreme $\tau_0=0$ endpoint ($s=1.50$), the residual DINO-S gap ($0.6054$ vs.\ $0.6379$) concentrates on \emph{Rococo} ($0.5387$ vs.\ $0.6390$): auditing all 120 cross-style \emph{Rococo} tasks shows that whereas DINO $4\times 4$ scene tokens route evenly across $8/8$ sub-schools ($H/\ln 8 = 0.8687$, max share $27.5\%$), grayscale ConvNeXt $4\times 4$ descriptors exhibit cosine hubness collapse onto cluster $c_7$ ($7/8$ active, $H/\ln 8 = 0.7102$, $45.8\%$ in $c_7$ scoring $0.4879$ DINO-S / $40.0\%$ Top-1), while non-hub clusters achieve $0.6614$ DINO-S / $100\%$ Top-1 ($c_3, n=16$) and $0.6092$ DINO-S / $95.7\%$ Top-1 ($c_5, n=23$).

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We demonstrated that \emph{more capacity can preserve the wrong information}, and resolved source-style backflow across conditioning, trajectory, and decoding spaces with \textbf{\method}. By combining a $\cnxParams$ ConvNeXt-Stage3 Stiefel Polar Quotient $Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}}$ ($91.33\%\!\to\!20.67\%$ Gram probe), a performance-hybrid \textbf{P/E-Core} transport engine (\textbf{\methodecore} $\ecoreParams$ 1-NFE + \textbf{\methodpcore} $\pcoreParams$ 6-step DPM-2M with $16\times 16$-only bottleneck conditioning and \texttt{Pool C} reversed pairs), and a $\decPrune$-pruned spectral-bottleneck decoder (\textbf{\methoddec}, $\decParams$), \textbf{\method} achieves full-spectrum Pareto dominance at $\pcoreColdBatchFps\text{--}\ecoreColdBatchFps$ cold-start RGB$\to$RGB ($\pcoreHotBatchFps\text{--}\ecoreHotBatchFps$ hot interactive) under $100\%$ blind cross-architecture evaluation.
