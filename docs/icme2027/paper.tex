\title{\method: Real-Time Quotient-Guided P/E-Core Transport for Full-Spectrum Style Transfer}

\author{\IEEEauthorblockN{Anonymous ICME Submission}
\IEEEauthorblockA{Paper ID: ****}}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Interactive artistic authoring demands a single real-time engine that sweeps smoothly across the full style--content spectrum, from structure-faithful local recoloring to abstract painterly reorganization. Across $300+$ controlled runs, we show why a single latent network fails to span both regimes: high-content editing is an injective $1$-to-$1$ transport problem requiring zero identity drift, whereas strong stylization is a stochastic $1$-to-many synthesis task. Scaling a self-paired flow by $260\times$ ($0.13\text{M}\!\to\!34.2\text{M}$) lifts unconditional generation ($20.0\%\!\to\!49.3\%$) yet degrades cross-style SDEdit ($10.67\%\!\to\!6.67\%$) due to Posterior Source-Lock. We propose \textbf{\method} (\textbf{C}ontinuous \textbf{L}atent \textbf{E}diting and \textbf{A}bstraction \textbf{V}elocity \textbf{E}ngine), a real-time performance-hybrid \textbf{Performance--Efficiency (P/E)-Core} latent transport framework controlled by a single knob $s \in [0, 1.50]$. First, to block source-style leakage without heavy ViT backbones, we project a $\cnxParams$ ConvNeXt-Tiny Stage-3 extractor ($0.985\,\text{ms/img}$) onto a \textbf{Stiefel Polar Quotient} ($Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}}$), clamping 2nd-order Gram matrices to $I_{32}$ and suppressing 5-way linear style predictability from $91.33\%$ to $20.67\%$ (chance floor). Second, a Hybrid Director dispatches regimes across asymmetric cores: a 1-NFE \textbf{\methodecore} ($\ecoreParams$) governs high-content editing ($s \in [0, 0.75]$) with exact $s=0$ identity ($\max\lvert\hat{z}-z_s\rvert=0.0$) and generates $1\,\text{ms}$ structural drafts $z_{\mathrm{E}}$, while a 6-step DPM-2M \textbf{\methodpcore} ($\pcoreParams$) takes over mid-to-extreme abstraction ($s \in [0.80, 1.50]$) via strict $16\times 16$ bottleneck conditioning and in-domain reversed real-painting pairs. Third, a $\decPrune$-pruned spectral-bottleneck decoder (\textbf{\methoddec}, $\decParams$) suppresses source-texture retraction ($\decGain\text{--}\decClosedGain$ Top-1) at $\decSpeedup$ speed. On a 750-task benchmark, \textbf{\method} spans $\sZeroFortyC \to \sOneFiftyC$ DINO-C and $\sZeroFortyTop \to \sOneFiftyTop$ CSD Top-1 at $\pcoreColdBatchFps\text{--}\ecoreColdBatchFps$ true cold-start RGB$\to$RGB ($\pcoreHotBatchFps\text{--}\ecoreHotBatchFps$ hot interactive)---$550\times\text{--}1{,}000\times$ faster than StyleGallery ($30.4\,\text{s/img}$) under $100\%$ blind cross-architecture evaluation.
\end{abstract}

Interactive multimedia authoring requires a real-time engine that sweeps continuously across the style--content spectrum, from subtle structure-faithful palette adaptation to bold, abstract brushstroke reorganization. Operating in the $1/8$-resolution latent space of a variational autoencoder (VAE)~\cite{rombach2022high} should in principle make full-spectrum control achievable within milliseconds. Yet existing methods remain split into two incompatible regimes (Fig.~\ref{fig:page1}): billion-parameter zero-shot diffusion and foundation editors~\cite{gao2024styleshot,chung2024style,he2026stylegallery,seedream2025} require $5\text{--}30.4\,\text{s/img}$ of iterative inversion or attention optimization, whereas lightweight feed-forward networks~\cite{deng2022stytr2,hong2023aespa,park2020cut,liu2024samam,afifi2021histogan} either under-stylize to protect contours ($<40\%$ CSD Top-1) or collapse spatial geometry under global moment shifts.

Through $300+$ controlled runs on a 750-task benchmark, we trace this gap to three intrinsic physical bottlenecks across the latent style transfer pipeline (Fig.~\ref{fig:paradoxes}):

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Single-Core Spectrum Conflict (The P/E-Core Necessity, Fig.~\ref{fig:paradoxes}a):} High-content editing ($s\le 0.75$) is an injective $1$-to-$1$ local transport problem requiring exact contour preservation and zero identity drift at $s=0$, whereas strong abstraction ($s\ge 0.80$) is a stochastic $1$-to-many synthesis task that must dissolve source micro-edges and reorganize macro planes. Forcing one self-paired flow~\cite{lipman2022flow,meng2021sdedit} to span both regimes triggers \emph{Posterior Source-Lock}: scaling capacity by $260\times$ ($0.13\text{M}\!\to\!34.2\text{M}$) lifts unconditional generation ($\tau_0=0$) from $20.0\%$ to $49.3\%$, yet sharpens reconstruction basins around $z_s$ and degrades cross-style SDEdit ($\tau_0=0.7$) from $10.67\%$ to $\mathbf{6.67\%}$ ($59\%$ predicted back as source style).
\item \textbf{Condition Style Leakage and Front-End Bottleneck:} Raw spatial feature tokens carry up to $91.33\%$ 5-way source style predictability in their 2nd-order Gram matrices, causing conditional flows to reconstruct source brushwork. Conversely, conditioning on $86\text{M}\text{--}304\text{M}$ DINOv2~\cite{oquab2023dinov2} ViT backbones burdens cold-start latency and couples the generator to ViT-based evaluation referees.
\item \textbf{Decoder Texture Retraction (Fig.~\ref{fig:paradoxes}b):} Over-parameterized $49.5\text{M}$ SD-VAE synthesis filters memorize natural-image texture priors that pull stylized latents back toward unedited appearance; pruning $\decPrune$ of decoder channels accelerates synthesis by $\decSpeedup$ while \emph{improving} cross-style Top-1 by $\decGain\text{--}\decClosedGain$.
\end{enumerate}

We resolve all three bottlenecks with \textbf{\method} (\textbf{C}ontinuous \textbf{L}atent \textbf{E}diting and \textbf{A}bstraction \textbf{V}elocity \textbf{E}ngine)---a name reflecting its dual action: \emph{cleaving} away source style via quotient projection, orthogonal luma--chroma splitting, P/E dual-core decoupling, and decoder pruning, while \emph{cleaving closely} to source geometry with zero drift in high-content editing (Fig.~\ref{fig:arch}):
(1)~\emph{Performance-Hybrid P/E-Core Transport \& Single-Knob Hybrid Director:} Inspired by performance-hybrid processor design, a 1-NFE efficiency core (\textbf{\methodecore}, $\ecoreParams$) governs $s\in[0,0.75]$ with exact mathematical identity at $s=0$ ($\max\lvert\hat{z}-z_s\rvert=0.0$) and generates $0.99\,\text{ms}$ structural drafts $z_{\mathrm{E}}(\lambda_{\mathrm{draft}}(s))$ for higher regimes. For $s\in[0.80,1.50]$, a 6-step DPM-2M performance core (\textbf{\methodpcore}, $\pcoreParams$) initializes from $z_{\mathrm{E}}$, restricts structural injection strictly to its $16\times 16$ bottleneck, and trains on $15\%$ in-domain reversed real-painting pairs (\texttt{Pool C}) to break Posterior Source-Lock.
(2)~\emph{Lightweight Convolutional Stiefel Polar Quotient \& Orthogonal Luma--Chroma Split:} A truncated $\cnxParams$ \textbf{ConvNeXt-Tiny Stage-3} front-end ($0.985\,\text{ms/img}$ at \texttt{b=16}) paired with Stiefel polar projection ($Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}}$) clamps sample Gram matrices to $I_{32}$ (error $1.95\times 10^{-4}$), driving 5-way Gram style predictability from $91.33\%$ to the $20.67\%$ chance floor alongside an orthogonal $P_Y\oplus P_C$ latent split and a 417-D $K=8$ sub-school style bank.
(3)~\emph{Spectral-Bottleneck Decoding (\methoddec) \& Blind Systems Audit:} Taylor-pruned \textbf{\methoddec} ($\decParams$) suppresses source retraction at $\decSpeedup$ speed. Across 750 tasks, we disclose both true cold-start RGB$\to$RGB ($\pcoreColdBatchFps\text{--}\ecoreColdBatchFps$, $\coldTotalParams$) and hot interactive ($\pcoreHotBatchFps\text{--}\ecoreHotBatchFps$, $\hotTotalParams$) budgets, and conduct a 6-arm $Q_{32}$ causal diagnosis plus paired DINO-vs.-ConvNeXt ablations that prove backbone-independence and quantify a $\sim 0.025$ homologous \emph{DINO-C} scale offset.

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Lightweight Feed-Forward Style Transfer.}
Classical neural style transfer evolved from iterative Gram optimization~\cite{gatys2016image,simonyan2014vgg} to feed-forward moment matching (AdaIN~\cite{huang2017arbitrary}, WCT~\cite{li2017universal}), attention/transformer networks (StyTr$^2$~\cite{deng2022stytr2}, AesPA-Net~\cite{hong2023aespa}, AesFA~\cite{kwon2024aesfa}), unpaired contrastive translation (CUT~\cite{park2020cut}), histogram alignment (Hist2Style~\cite{afifi2021histogan}), and state-space models (SaMam~\cite{liu2024samam}, SaMST~\cite{liu2024samst}). While compact ($0.1\text{M}\text{--}48\text{M}$ parameters), global moment matching degrades object boundaries, whereas conservative translators protect contours by barely shifting artistic style. \textbf{\method} overcomes this trade-off via orthogonal quotient--fiber conditioning and asymmetric P/E-Core specialization.

\textbf{Zero-Shot Diffusion Editors, Latent Bridges, and Decoder Pruning.}
Large pretrained diffusion and foundation models~\cite{meng2021sdedit,zhang2023inst,ye2023ipadapter,hertz2024style,chung2024style,gao2024styleshot,he2026stylegallery,seedream2025} synthesize vivid textures at heavy compute cost ($0.95\text{B}\text{--}15\text{B}$ parameters, $5\text{--}30.4\,\text{s/img}$), including attention injection (StyleID~\cite{chung2024style}, StyleShot~\cite{gao2024styleshot}), commercial multimodal generation (Seedream~4.5~\cite{seedream2025}), and 150-step DINOv2-guided inversion/matching (StyleGallery~\cite{he2026stylegallery}). Latent flow bridges~\cite{lipman2022flow,liu2022rectified,chadebec2025lbm,kim2024latentsb} and spatial adapters~\cite{zhang2023controlnet,park2019spade} reduce step counts, yet self-paired $64\times 64$ conditioning locks onto source strokes. Moreover, unlike reconstruction-driven VAE pruning~\cite{sadat2024litevae,lin2023favae,molchanov2019importance}, \textbf{\methoddec} exploits structured channel pruning as a spectral regularizer that blocks source-texture retraction.

\section{Theory and Methodology}
\label{sec:method}

Given an unlabeled source image $x_s \in [0,1]^{3\times H\times W}$ with VAE latent $z_s = \mathcal{E}(x_s) \in \R^{4\times 64\times 64}$ ($H=W=512$) and target movement $y_t \in \{1,\dots,5\}$ under single knob $s \in [0, 1.50]$, \textbf{\method} synthesizes $\hat{x}_{s\to y_t}(s) = \mathcal{D}_\star(\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{P/E}}(z_s, \Pi_\perp^{\mathrm{cnx}}(x_s), \Pi_\parallel^{\mathrm{cnx}}(x_s, y_t); s))$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:arch}).

\subsection{Posterior Source-Lock in Self-Paired Flows}
\label{sec:sourcelock}

In standard self-paired flow matching~\cite{lipman2022flow}, velocity field $v_\theta(z_\tau,\tau,y)$ is trained on $z_\tau = (1-\tau)\epsilon + \tau z$. At test time, SDEdit~\cite{meng2021sdedit} initializes at $z_{\tau_0} = (1-\tau_0)\epsilon + \tau_0 z_s$ ($z_s\sim\Prob_{y_s}$) under target $y_t \neq y_s$.

\begin{proposition}[Posterior Source-Lock]
\label{prop:sourcelock}
With label dropout $p_{\mathrm{drop}}\in(0,1)$, the Bayes-optimal velocity at $(z_{\tau_0},\tau_0,y_t)$ is $\frac{\hat{z}_{\tau_0}(y_t)-z_{\tau_0}}{1-\tau_0}$, where the posterior expectation under $\tilde{\Prob}_{y_t}=(1-\eta)\Prob_{y_t}+\eta\sum_k\pi_k\Prob_k$ is
\begin{equation}
\hat{z}_{\tau_0}(y_t) = \frac{\int_{\mathcal{Z}} z \, \exp\!\Big(-\frac{\norm{z_{\tau_0} - \tau_0 z}_2^2}{2(1-\tau_0)^2}\Big) d\tilde{\Prob}_{y_t}(z)}{\int_{\mathcal{Z}} \exp\!\Big(-\frac{\norm{z_{\tau_0} - \tau_0 z}_2^2}{2(1-\tau_0)^2}\Big) d\tilde{\Prob}_{y_t}(z)}.
\label{eq:posterior-lock}
\end{equation}
For $\Delta_{s\to t}(z_s)=\mathrm{dist}(z_s,\mathrm{supp}(\Prob_{y_t}))^2$, whenever $\frac{\tau_0^2\Delta_{s\to t}(z_s)}{2(1-\tau_0)^2} \gg \log\frac{1-\eta}{\eta\pi_{y_s}}$, the Gaussian kernel concentrates exponentially on $z_s\in\mathrm{supp}(\Prob_{y_s})$, yielding $\norm{\hat{z}_{\tau_0}(y_t)-z_s}_2=\mathcal{O}(1-\tau_0)$ regardless of $y_t$.
\end{proposition}
Prop.~\ref{prop:sourcelock} proves why a single network cannot span the full spectrum: high $\tau_0$ locks a flow onto $z_s$, whereas lowering $\tau_0$ without a stylistically pre-shifted draft destroys fine content geometry, motivating our asymmetric P/E-Core decomposition.

\subsection{Quotient-Guided Orthogonal Conditioning}
\label{sec:quotient}

To prevent source-style leakage through spatial conditioning while maintaining sub-millisecond front-end latency, we construct an orthogonal quotient--fiber representation:

\textbf{1) Luminance Structure $C_{64}$ and Coarse $Y_{16}$:}
From ITU-R BT.601 grayscale luminance $Y(x)=w_Y^\top x$ downsampled to $Y_{64}$ with local residual $\Delta_k Y=Y_{64}-\mathcal{A}_k(Y_{64})$ ($k\in\{5,13\}$), gradient-safe Local Contrast Normalization is
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{LCN}_k(Y_{64}) = \tfrac{1}{3}\mathrm{clip}\!\left(\frac{\Delta_k Y}{\sqrt{\max(\mathcal{A}_k(\Delta_k Y^2),\delta_0)} + \varepsilon_k},-3,3\right),
\label{eq:lcn}
\end{equation}
($\delta_0=10^{-8}$), stacked with 95th-percentile Sobel and RMS Laplacian maps into $C_{64}(x)\in[-1,1]^{4\times 64\times 64}$ for \textbf{\methodecore}, and pooled to $16\times 16$ $\mathrm{LCN}_3(Y_{16})\in[-1,1]^{1\times 16\times 16}$ (32px blocks, zero micro-stroke edges) for \textbf{\methodpcore}.

\textbf{2) ConvNeXt-Stage3 Stiefel Polar Quotient $Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}}$:}
We truncate an ImageNet-pretrained ConvNeXt-Tiny backbone at Stage~3 (\texttt{features[:6]}, $\cnxParams$ parameters, $D=384$ at stride~$16$). Feeding $256\times 256$ grayscale $[Y,Y,Y]$ yields spatial tokens $P_{\mathrm{cnx}}\in\R^{384\times 256}$ in $\mathbf{0.985\,\text{ms/img}}$ (\texttt{b=16}), avoiding both $86\text{M}+$ ViT latency and evaluator homology. Because standard diagonal channel whitening leaves off-diagonal correlations intact ($91.33\%$ 5-way Gram style leakage), we project onto the Stiefel manifold:

\begin{proposition}[Stiefel Polar Quotient \& Zero 2nd-Order Gram Predictability]
\label{prop:stiefel}
Let $W_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}} \in \R^{384\times 32}$ be the principal semantic basis fit on training grayscale tokens. For spatially centered projection $Z = (W_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}})^\top P_{\mathrm{cnx}}(I_N - \frac{1}{N}\mathbf{1}\mathbf{1}^\top) \in \R^{32\times N}$ ($N=256$) with compact SVD $Z = U \Sigma V^\top$ ($U\in O(32), V^\top V = I_{32}$), the Stiefel Polar Quotient
\begin{equation}
Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}}(x) = \sqrt{N}\, U V^\top \in \R^{32\times 16\times 16}
\label{eq:stiefel-q}
\end{equation}
is the unique Frobenius-nearest tensor to $Z$ satisfying exact zero spatial mean ($Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}}\mathbf{1} \equiv \mathbf{0}$) and exact identity sample Gram matrix ($\frac{1}{N} Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}} (Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}})^\top \equiv I_{32}$).
\end{proposition}
On 300 holdout paintings, $Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}}$ achieves a maximum Gram identity numerical error of $1.95\times 10^{-4}$ and slashes 5-way Gram style probe accuracy from $91.33\%$ to $\mathbf{20.67\%}$ (matching the $20.0\%$ uniform random baseline).

\textbf{3) 417-D $K=8$ Sub-School Style Bank and Scene Router:}
To capture fine intra-movement sub-styles, we extract $f_{417}(x) = [\text{Oklab}_{27}(x) \;\|\; \text{Haar}_{6}(x) \;\|\; \mu_{\mathrm{RGB}}^{\mathrm{ConvNeXt\text{-}S3}}(x)] \in \R^{417}$, combining 27-D Oklab color moments/quantiles/covariances, 6-D 3-level Haar wavelet log-energy ratios, and the 384-D global spatial mean of RGB ConvNeXt-Stage3 features. Within each style's $k$-NN-30 core training manifold, spherical $k$-means partitions exemplars into $K=8$ sub-schools with centroids $\{u_{y_t,k}^{\mathrm{cnx}}\}_{k=1}^8 \subset \R^{417}$ and $4\times 4$-pooled grayscale scene descriptors $\{s_{y_t,k}^{\mathrm{cnx}}\}_{k=1}^8 \subset \R^{6144}$. At inference ($0.025\,\text{ms/img}$), source scene layout $s(x_s)=\mathrm{norm}(\mathrm{AvgPool}_{4\times 4}(P_{\mathrm{cnx}}(x_s)))$ routes to the compatible target sub-school $k^\star = \arg\max_k \langle s(x_s), s_{y_t,k}^{\mathrm{cnx}}\rangle$.

\subsection{Performance-Hybrid P/E-Core Transport and \methoddec}
\label{sec:transport}

Regressing $Y_{64}\approx w_{\mathrm{luma}}^\top z + b_{\mathrm{luma}}$ splits latent space $\R^4$ orthogonally into 1-D structural luminance $P_Y = \hat{w}_Y\hat{w}_Y^\top$ and 3-D chrominance $P_C = I_4 - P_Y$.

\textbf{1) Orthogonal Luma--Chroma Split and \methodecore{} ($\ecoreParams$, 1-NFE, $s \in [0, 0.75]$):}
In the high-content regime, \textbf{\methodecore} executes a single feed-forward pass ($\mathbf{0.986\,\text{ms/img}}$ network, $\ecoreColdBatchMs$ cold RGB$\to$RGB) using a gated hybrid U-Net conditioned on $(z_s, C_{64}, Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}}, y_t, \lambda)$ and class-level 417-D statistics:
\begin{equation}
z_{\mathrm{E}}(x_s, y_t; \lambda) \!=\! z_0^\perp(z_s, y_t; \lambda) + \lambda \Delta_{\theta_{\mathrm{E}}}\!\big(z_s, \gamma_{\mathrm{skip}}(\lambda) C_{64}, Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}}, y_t\big),
\label{eq:ecore}
\end{equation}
where $z_0^\perp(z_s,y_t;0)\equiv z_s$ and encoder skip gate $\gamma_{\mathrm{skip}}(\lambda)=\max(0, 1-0.8\lambda)$ progressively releases source high-frequency contours as $\lambda$ increases. At $\lambda=0$ ($s=0$), Eq.~\eqref{eq:ecore} enforces \textbf{exact mathematical identity} $\hat{z}\equiv z_s$ (measured maximum absolute latent drift $\mathbf{0.0}$). For $s > 0.75$, \textbf{\methodecore} acts as a $0.99\,\text{ms}$ draft generator $z_{\mathrm{E}}(\lambda_{\mathrm{draft}}(s))$ that pre-shifts chrominance and coarse brushwork before flow integration.

\textbf{2) \methodpcore{} ($\pcoreParams$, 6-Step DPM-2M Flow, $s \in [0.80, 1.50]$):}
In the abstraction regime, \textbf{\methodpcore} initializes at $z_{\tau_0} = (1-\tau_0(s))\epsilon + \tau_0(s) z_{\mathrm{E}}(x_s, y_t; \lambda_{\mathrm{draft}}(s))$ (with $\tau_0(1.50)=0$ for pure generative synthesis) and integrates 6 steps of 2nd-order multistep DPM-Solver++ (\texttt{dpm2m}) with 4-branch decoupled classifier-free guidance:
\begin{equation}
v_{\mathrm{CFG}} = v_{00} + w_c(v_{0c}\!-\!v_{00}) + w_s(v_{sc}\!-\!v_{0c}) + w_{sp}(v_{s0}\!-\!v_{00}).
\label{eq:pcore-cfg}
\end{equation}
To eliminate $64\times 64$ source-edge locking, structural tokens $(\mathrm{LCN}_3(Y_{16}), \gamma_{\mathrm{layout}}(s) Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}})$ are injected \emph{exclusively at the $16\times 16$ bottleneck} (removing $64^2/32^2$ structural skips), while $32\times 32$ and $64\times 64$ synthesis blocks are modulated solely by $u_{y_t,k^\star}^{\mathrm{cnx}}$. To break Posterior Source-Lock during training ($3{,}500$ steps, $27.1\,\text{min}$ on one RTX~4090), each batch mixes $85\%$ $k$-NN-30 core paintings with $15\%$ curated \textbf{in-domain reversed real-painting pairs (\texttt{Pool C})}---pairing real ground-truth targets ($z_{\mathrm{real}}, y_t$) from under-represented movements (600 ER + 600 Uki) with de-styled structural tokens $(\mathrm{LCN}_3(Y_{16}), Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}})$ extracted from synthetic cross-style sources---jointly supervised by high-$\tau$ pixel-space sliced Wasserstein distance (SWD) through \textbf{\methoddec}.

\textbf{3) Single-Knob Hybrid Director and \methoddec{} ($\decParams$):}
A lightweight Hybrid Director maps $s\in[0,1.50]$ deterministically to \textbf{\methodecore} ($s\le 0.75$) or \textbf{\methodpcore} ($s\ge 0.80$, calibrating $\tau_0, \lambda_{\mathrm{draft}}, \gamma_{\mathrm{layout}}, w_s$). Finally, structured Taylor channel pruning~\cite{molchanov2019importance} compresses the $49.49\text{M}$ SD-VAE decoder by $\decPrune$ to widths $[64,128,192,256]$ (\textbf{\methoddec}, $\decParams$), serving as a spectral bottleneck that blocks source-texture retraction ($\decGain\text{--}\decClosedGain$ Top-1) at $\decSpeedup$ speed.

\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Benchmark, Stratified Baselines, and Dual-Latency Protocol}

We evaluate at $512\times 512$ across five WikiArt movements~\cite{wright2022artfid}: \emph{Early Renaissance} (ER), \emph{Impressionism} (Imp), \emph{Minimalism} (Min), \emph{Rococo} (Roc), and \emph{Ukiyo-e} (Uki). \subsection{Full-Spectrum Pareto Dominance and Representation-Space Affinity Analysis}

Table~\ref{tab:main}, Fig.~\ref{fig:page1}, and Fig.~\ref{fig:qual} establish three key empirical results across the style--content--speed spectrum:

\textbf{1) Dominance Over Tier~1 Lightweight Baselines and High-Content Foundation Models (\methodecore):}
Running in $\ecoreColdBatchMs$ cold RGB$\to$RGB ($\ecoreColdBatchFps$ at \texttt{b=16}; $\ecoreColdSingleMs / \ecoreColdSingleFps$ at \texttt{b=1}; $\ecoreHotBatchMs / \ecoreHotBatchFps$ hot interactive) with $98.0\%\text{--}98.7\%$ Same150 identity preservation, 1-NFE \textbf{\methodecore} strictly dominates every Tier~1 baseline at matched content levels: at $s=0.40$ ($\sZeroFortyC$ DINO-C, $\sZeroFortyS$ DINO-S, $\sZeroFortyTop$ Top-1, $0.3503$ LPIPS) it beats CUT ($0.8435$ C, $5.7\%$ Top-1) and SaMam ($0.8239$ C, $2.5\%$ Top-1) on all metrics; at $s=0.75$ ($\sZeroSeventyFiveC$ DINO-C, $\sZeroSeventyFiveS$ DINO-S, $\sZeroSeventyFiveTop$ Top-1, $0.4734$ LPIPS) it simultaneously outperforms StyTr$^2$ ($39.5\%$, $3.0\,\text{s}$), Seedream~4.5 ($37.3\%$, $20.0\,\text{s}$), and $15\text{B}$ Qwen-Image-2.1-Turbo ($42.2\%$, $16.1\,\text{s}$) across all five style and content metrics while running $100\times\text{--}660\times$ faster. At $s=1.00$, standalone 1-NFE \textbf{\methodecore} reaches $\mathbf{\ecoreMaxTop}$ Top-1 ($1.17\%$ wrong-label control, $\ecoreCausalGap$ causal specificity).

\textbf{2) Mid-Spectrum Calibration and Neutral Representation-Space Affinity Comparison vs.\ StyleGallery (\methodpcore):}
Across $s=0.80\text{--}0.85$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:page1}), 6-step \textbf{\methodpcore} ($\pcoreColdBatchMs$ cold RGB$\to$RGB, $\pcoreColdBatchFps$) bridges smoothly from \textbf{\methodecore}, reaching $\sZeroEightyFiveC$ DINO-C, $\sZeroEightyFiveS$ DINO-S, $0.5300$ CSLS, and $\mathbf{\sZeroEightyFiveTop}$ CSD Top-1 at $s=0.85$ ($+7.83\text{pp}$ Top-1 and $+0.0767$ CSLS over uncalibrated handoff knots, $2.17\%$ wrong-label Top-1, $96.0\%$ Same150). Comparing $s=0.85$ ($\sZeroEightyFiveC$ C) against Tier~2 StyleGallery ($0.6113$ C, $0.5604$ DINO-S, $52.50\%$ Top-1, $30.4\,\text{s/img}$) reveals two objective phenomena:
(i)~\emph{Representation-Space Affinity vs.\ Disentangled Style Accuracy:} StyleGallery optimizes regional feature matching inside frozen DINOv2 space over 150 diffusion steps, naturally exhibiting strong \emph{representation-space affinity} with the DINOv2-based \emph{DINO-S} referee ($0.5604$ vs.\ $0.5266$). Conversely, on the orthogonal, architecture-independent \emph{CSD Top-1} referee, StyleGallery scores $52.50\%$, whereas our \textbf{\methodpcore} ($s=0.85$) achieves $\mathbf{62.33\%}$ ($\mathbf{+9.83\text{pp}}$) and 1-NFE \textbf{\methodecore} ($s=1.00$) achieves $\mathbf{67.17\%}$ ($\mathbf{+14.67\text{pp}}$) at \textbf{$550\times\text{--}1{,}000\times$ higher cold-start throughput}.
(ii)~\emph{Figurative Alignment vs.\ Structure-Preserving Minimalism Trade-Off:} At $C\approx 0.60$, our unified schedule preserves object contours on \emph{Minimalism} ($0.5510$ DINO-C, $96.67\%$ Top-1, $0.3337$ DINO-S) rather than erasing geometry into blank canvas, while figurative movements already reach $0.5599\text{--}0.6582$ DINO-S (\emph{Imp} $0.6582$, \emph{Roc} $0.5734$, \emph{ER} $0.5599$). In the strong-abstraction regime ($s=1.00\text{--}1.50$), \textbf{\methodpcore} reaches $\mathbf{0.5628\text{--}0.6054}$ DINO-S Max ($0.5032\text{--}0.5461$ Top-5 Mean) and $\mathbf{75.17\%\text{--}80.17\%}$ Top-1 ($\sOneFiftyCausal$ causal gap), surpassing both StyleGallery ($0.5604$) and $2.4\text{B}$ StyleShot ($0.5750$).

\textbf{2) Error Attribution of the $s=1.50$ Tail Drop via $K=8$ Hubness Audit (Table~\ref{tab:system}B):}
At the extreme $\tau_0=0$ endpoint ($s=1.50$), ConvNeXt-conditioned \textbf{\methodpcore} reaches $0.6054$ DINO-S vs.\ $0.6379$ for its DINO-conditioned twin, with the gap concentrated on \emph{Rococo} ($0.5387$ vs.\ $0.6390$). \textbf{3) Scope and Limitations:}
\textbf{\method} amortizes a 5-movement repertoire to achieve $18.2\text{--}33.1\,\text{img/s}$ cold-start throughput under blind evaluation. Extending our 417-D ConvNeXt fiber and \texttt{Pool C} reversed-pair training to open-domain 1-shot arbitrary reference images without test-time style indices is a natural next step.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduced \textbf{\method}, a real-time performance-hybrid \textbf{P/E-Core} latent transport framework that resolves the mathematical conflict between structure-faithful editing and generative brushstroke abstraction. Combining a $\cnxParams$ ConvNeXt-Stage3 Stiefel Polar Quotient $Q_{32}^{\mathrm{cnx}}$ ($91.33\%\!\to\!20.67\%$ Gram probe), a 1-NFE \textbf{\methodecore} ($\ecoreParams$, exact $s=0$ identity and $0.99\,\text{ms}$ structural drafts), a 6-step DPM-2M \textbf{\methodpcore} ($\pcoreParams$, strict $16\times 16$ bottleneck conditioning and in-domain reversed real-painting pairs), and a $\decPrune$-pruned \textbf{\methoddec} ($\decParams$), \textbf{\method} achieves full-spectrum Pareto dominance at $\pcoreColdBatchFps\text{--}\ecoreColdBatchFps$ cold-start RGB$\to$RGB ($\pcoreHotBatchFps\text{--}\ecoreHotBatchFps$ hot interactive) under $100\%$ blind cross-architecture evaluation.
