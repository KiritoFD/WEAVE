\begin{abstract}
Operating in the compressed latent space of a frozen variational autoencoder promises millisecond-scale artistic style transfer, yet existing editors split into two extremes: multi-billion-parameter diffusion models achieve strong stylization only by distorting scene geometry over seconds of iterative optimization, whereas compact translators stall near identity or suffer perceptual artifacts. Through a controlled 250-run audit on 750 cross- and same-style pairs, we trace this barrier to \emph{Source-Style Lock}: self-paired trajectories, isotropic latent constraints, and over-parameterized reconstruction decoders preserve nuisance source-domain mutual information that pulls edited latents back toward the input style. Strikingly, scaling a self-paired latent flow network by $260\times$ ($0.13\text{M}\!\to\!34.2\text{M}$) boosts unconditional generation from $20.0\%$ to $49.3\%$, yet monotonically \emph{degrades} cross-style SDEdit from $10.67\%$ to $6.67\%$ ($59\%$ source-style lock). Furthermore, we prove that standard diagonal whitening retains $92.3\%$ channel Gram style leakage.

We propose \textbf{\method} (\emph{Projected Invariant Style Mapping}), a modular three-operator factorization $\mathcal{D}_\star \circ \mathcal{T}_{s\to t} \circ (\Pi_\perp,\Pi_\parallel)$ built on \emph{Orthogonal Quotient--Fiber Duality}. First, complementary projections decompose any input $x_s$ into a Stiefel polar structural quotient $\Pi_\perp(x_s)$ ($18.3\%$ style leakage vs.\ $20\%$ uniform chance; exact $I_k$ Gram identity) and its complementary style fiber $\Pi_\parallel(x_s)$ ($97.3\%$ holdout style accuracy), enabling a \emph{source-label-free universal chord} $e(x_s\!\to\!\tau)=u_\tau-\phi(\Pi_\parallel(x_s))$ for both class prototypes and 1-shot exemplars. Second, quotient-guided latent transport $\mathcal{T}$ offers two unified operational regimes: a $\fastParams$ spatially routed real-time network (\textbf{\method}, $\fastMs$, $\fastFps$ on RTX~4090) and an exemplar-conditioned generative flow (\textbf{\methodflow}, $\flowMs$). Third, $\decPrune$ structured channel pruning of the SD-VAE decoder (\textbf{\methoddec}, $\decParams$) acts as a spectral bottleneck regularizer that accelerates decoding by $\decSpeedup$ while \emph{increasing} cross-style accuracy by $\decGain$ ($p<0.01$). On 750 WikiArt benchmark tasks, real-time \textbf{\method} reaches $\fastTopOne$ CSD Top-1 ($\fastWrongTopOne$ under counterfactual wrong labels; $+43.5\text{pp}$ causal specificity), unconditionally dominating Seedream~4.5 ($37.30\%$) and StyTr$^2$ ($39.50\%$) on all style, content, and distortion metrics at $117\times\text{--}178\times$ higher speed. Concurrently, \textbf{\methodflow} reaches $\flowBalDinoS\text{--}\flowDinoS$ DINO-S, $\flowBalAff\text{--}\flowAff$ Style Affinity, and $\flowBalTopOne\text{--}\flowTopOne$ Top-1—surpassing diffusion cross-attention StyleGallery ($0.5604$ DINO-S, $0.6432$ Affinity, $52.50\%$ Top-1) at $\mathbf{226\times}$ higher throughput while running under $4.1\,\text{GB}$ VRAM on an RTX~3060.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Compressing images into the latent space of a frozen variational autoencoder (VAE)~\cite{rombach2022high} reduces spatial resolution by $8\times$, suggesting that conditional style transfer—rendering an unlabeled input $x_s$ in target style $\tau$ while preserving scene geometry—should be solvable in millisecond-scale latent forward passes. Yet existing systems remain polarized (Fig.~\ref{fig:page1}). Diffusion editors~\cite{gao2024styleshot,chung2024style,hertz2024style,he2026stylegallery} achieve strong brushstroke similarity only by running iterative latent optimization or gigabyte U-Nets over $5\text{--}30\,\text{s/img}$, whereas compact transformer, contrastive, and Mamba translators~\cite{deng2022stytr2,park2020cut,liu2024samam} either introduce heavy geometric distortion or stay near identity.

Why do scaling and distillation stall in reconstruction-trained latent spaces? Across $250+$ controlled runs on a unified 750-task benchmark, we trace the failure to \textbf{Reconstruction--Transport Antagonism}: trajectories, latent metrics, and decoders optimized for \emph{self-reconstruction} preserve source-domain mutual information $I(\cdot\,;Y_s\mid C)$, creating \textbf{Source-Style Lock} across three interlocking paradoxes (Fig.~\ref{fig:paradoxes}):

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Trajectory Lock ($260\times$ Scaling Paradox, Fig.~\ref{fig:paradoxes}a):} Scaling a self-paired rectified flow~\cite{lipman2022flow,meng2021sdedit} by $260\times$ ($0.13\text{M}\!\to\!34.2\text{M}$) lifts pure noise generation ($\tau_0=0$) Top-1 from $20.0\%$ to $49.3\%$, yet monotonically \emph{lowers} SDEdit transfer ($\tau_0=0.7$) from $10.67\%$ to $\mathbf{6.67\%}$. Even $\tau_0=0$ pure-noise flows conditioned on chrominance-free edges stall at $18.67\%$ because self-paired training ($x_s\mapsto x_s$) inverts 2D stroke cadence back into $y_s$.
\item \textbf{Decoder Retraction \& Isotropic Palette Freeze (Fig.~\ref{fig:paradoxes}b):} Full $49.5\text{M}$ SD-VAE synthesis filters memorize natural/source priors; pruning $\decPrune$ of channels (\textbf{\methoddec}, $\decParams$) yields $\decSpeedup$ speedup and $\mathbf{\decGain}$ higher Top-1 ($p<0.01$). Simultaneously, standard GroupNorm latent critics normalize out palette moments while isotropic Euclidean trust balls penalize 3-D latent chrominance shifts as ``content loss,'' starving woodblock styles (Ukiyo-e $<1\%$).
\item \textbf{Evaluation Mirage (Fig.~\ref{fig:paradoxes}c):} Across $143$ models, ArtFID~\cite{wright2022artfid} correlates negatively with style ($\rho=-0.16$), while unanchored post-hoc scalers score \emph{higher} under counterfactual \emph{wrong} target labels than true ones ($13.0\%$ vs.\ $12.3\%$).
\end{enumerate}

To break Source-Style Lock without test-time source labels, \textbf{\method} (\emph{Projected Invariant Style Mapping}) introduces \textbf{Orthogonal Quotient--Fiber Duality} $(\Pi_\perp,\Pi_\parallel)$. While $\Pi_\perp(x_s)$ projects onto chrominance-free contrast and a Stiefel polar orthogonalized semantic layout with exact zero 2nd-order Gram leakage ($18.3\%$ style probe), its exact complement $\Pi_\parallel(x_s)$ collects the discarded color quantiles and DINO spatial moments ($97.3\%$ style accuracy) to form a \emph{universal source-free chord} $e(x_s\!\to\!\tau)=u_\tau-\phi(\Pi_\parallel(x_s))$. Splitting $\R^4=\mathrm{span}(\hat{w}_Y)\oplus\hat{w}_Y^\perp$ pairs tight luminance-contrast preservation with wide chrominance freedom under an un-normalized VGG+Oklab teacher through \textbf{\methoddec}. This framework yields two complementary operational regimes: a real-time feed-forward network (\textbf{\method}, $\fastMs$) and an exemplar-conditioned generative flow (\textbf{\methodflow}, $\flowMs$).

\textbf{Contributions.}
(1)~\emph{Source-Lock Theory \& Dual-Gate Audit:} We prove posterior source-lock in self-paired flows (Prop.~\ref{prop:sourcelock}) and establish Identity--Target validity plus counterfactual wrong-label causal gates.
(2)~\emph{Quotient--Fiber Duality \& Stiefel Orthogonality:} We prove that standard diagonal whitening retains $92.3\%$ Gram style leakage and introduce the Stiefel polar quotient (Prop.~\ref{prop:stiefel}) achieving exact zero 2nd-order Gram leakage ($20.0\%$ chance level), enabling source-label-free class and 1-shot exemplar chords (Prop.~\ref{prop:antisym}).
(3)~\emph{Decisive State-of-the-Art Dominance:} Operating in real time ($\fastMs$, $\fastFps$), \textbf{\method} ($\fastTopOne$) unconditionally surpasses Seedream~4.5 ($37.30\%$) and StyTr$^2$ ($39.50\%$) on all style, content, and distortion metrics. In the generative brushstroke regime, \textbf{\methodflow} reaches $\flowDinoS$ DINO-S, $\flowAff$ Affinity, and $\flowTopOne$ Top-1—surpassing diffusion cross-attention StyleGallery ($0.5604$ DINO-S, $0.6432$ Affinity, $52.50\%$ Top-1) at $\mathbf{226\times}$ higher throughput while executing under $4.1\,\text{GB}$ VRAM on an RTX~3060.

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\textbf{Neural Style Transfer.}
Classical NST progressed from Gram optimization~\cite{gatys2016image,simonyan2014vgg} to global moment transforms (AdaIN~\cite{huang2017arbitrary}, WCT~\cite{li2017universal}), attention/transformers (StyTr$^2$~\cite{deng2022stytr2}, AesPA-Net~\cite{hong2023aespa}, AesFA~\cite{kwon2024aesfa}), contrastive translation (CUT~\cite{park2020cut}), and Mamba models (SaMam~\cite{liu2024samam}, SaMST~\cite{liu2024samst}). Moment transforms often corrupt contours, while Mamba/contrastive models preserve content by under-stylizing ($<6\%$ CSD Top-1). \textbf{\method} routes semantic tokens in $64\times 64$ latents to reach $44.3\%\text{--}64.0\%$ Top-1.

\textbf{Latent Diffusion, Flows, and Tokenizers.}
Diffusion editors~\cite{meng2021sdedit,zhang2023inst,ye2023ipadapter,hertz2024style,chung2024style,gao2024styleshot,he2026stylegallery} either overwrite scene geometry or require multi-stage feature clustering and per-image attention optimization ($5\text{--}30\,\text{s/img}$); latent bridges~\cite{lipman2022flow,liu2022rectified,chadebec2025lbm,kim2024latentsb} and spatial adapters~\cite{zhang2023controlnet,park2019spade} shorten sampling, yet self-paired $64\times 64$ edge conditioning locks onto source brushstrokes. Unlike reconstruction-focused VAEs~\cite{sadat2024litevae,lin2023favae} or pure compression pruning~\cite{molchanov2019importance}, \textbf{\methoddec} leverages structured pruning both to suppress source-texture retraction and to enable fast differentiable pixel-space trajectory synthesis. For evaluation, we pair CSD~\cite{somepalli2024csd}, DINOv2~\cite{oquab2023dinov2}, CLIP~\cite{radford2021clip}, and LPIPS~\cite{zhang2018unreasonable} with validity~\cite{ioannou2024evaluation} and counterfactual causal gates.

\subsection{Posterior Source-Lock in Self-Paired Flows}
\label{sec:sourcelock}

In self-paired flow matching~\cite{lipman2022flow}, $v_\theta(z_\tau,\tau,y)$ is trained on $z_\tau = (1-\tau)\epsilon + \tau z$. At inference, SDEdit~\cite{meng2021sdedit} initializes at $z_{\tau_0} = (1-\tau_0)\epsilon + \tau_0 z_s$ ($z_s\sim\Prob_{y_s}$) under target $y_t \neq y_s$.

\begin{proposition}[Posterior Source-Lock]
\label{prop:sourcelock}
With label dropout $p_{\mathrm{drop}}\in(0,1)$, the Bayes-optimal velocity at $(z_{\tau_0},\tau_0,y_t)$ is $\frac{\hat{z}_{\tau_0}(y_t)-z_{\tau_0}}{1-\tau_0}$, with posterior expectation under $\tilde{\Prob}_{y_t}=(1-\eta)\Prob_{y_t}+\eta\sum_k\pi_k\Prob_k$:
\begin{equation}
\hat{z}_{\tau_0}(y_t) = \frac{\int_{\mathcal{Z}} z \, \exp\!\Big(-\frac{\norm{z_{\tau_0} - \tau_0 z}_2^2}{2(1-\tau_0)^2}\Big) d\tilde{\Prob}_{y_t}(z)}{\int_{\mathcal{Z}} \exp\!\Big(-\frac{\norm{z_{\tau_0} - \tau_0 z}_2^2}{2(1-\tau_0)^2}\Big) d\tilde{\Prob}_{y_t}(z)}.
\label{eq:posterior-lock}
\end{equation}
For $\Delta_{s\to t}(z_s)=\mathrm{dist}(z_s,\mathrm{supp}(\Prob_{y_t}))^2$, whenever $\frac{\tau_0^2\Delta_{s\to t}(z_s)}{2(1-\tau_0)^2} \gg \log\frac{1-\eta}{\eta\pi_{y_s}}$, the Gaussian kernel concentrates exponentially on $z_s\in\mathrm{supp}(\Prob_{y_s})$, giving $\norm{\hat{z}_{\tau_0}(y_t)-z_s}_2=\mathcal{O}(1-\tau_0)$ regardless of $y_t$.
\end{proposition}
Higher capacity ($0.13\text{M}\!\to\!34.2\text{M}$) sharpens local score curvature around $z_{\tau_0}$, worsening source-lock ($10.67\%\!\to\!6.67\%$), while $\tau_0=0$ self-paired edge conditioning still leaks 2D stroke cadence ($18.67\%$ ceiling).

\subsection{Operator I: Orthogonal Quotient--Fiber Duality $(\Pi_\perp, \Pi_\parallel)$}
\label{sec:quotient}

Global style acts via $\mathcal{G}_{\mathrm{style}} = \ker(w_Y^\top) \times (\R^D\rtimes\R_+^D)$, combining RGB chrominance orthogonal to luminance $Y(x)=w_Y^\top x$ and spatial affine moments~\cite{huang2017arbitrary}. We decompose $x$ into orthogonal quotient and fiber coordinates:

\textbf{1) Invariant Structural Quotient $\Pi_\perp(x)=(C_{64},Q_k)$:}
Downsampling smoothed $Y(x)$ to $Y_{64}$ with $\Delta_k Y=Y_{64}-\mathcal{A}_k(Y_{64})$ ($k\in\{5,13\}$), gradient-safe Local Contrast Normalization is
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{LCN}_k(Y_{64}) = \tfrac{1}{3}\mathrm{clip}\!\left(\frac{\Delta_k Y}{\sqrt{\max(\mathcal{A}_k(\Delta_k Y^2),\delta_0)} + \varepsilon_k},-3,3\right),
\label{eq:lcn}
\end{equation}
with $\delta_0=10^{-8}$, forming $C_{64}(x)\in[-1,1]^{4\times 64^2}$ alongside quantile Sobel and RMS Laplacian maps. To extract semantic tokens without leaking source style, we extract DINOv2-small~\cite{oquab2023dinov2} tokens $P\in\R^{D\times N}$ ($D=384, N=256$) from grayscale $[Y,Y,Y]$. Standard diagonal whitening normalizes channels individually:
\begin{equation}
\hat{P}_d = \frac{P_d - \mu_{\mathrm{sp}}(P_d)}{\sigma_{\mathrm{sp}}(P_d) + \varepsilon}.
\label{eq:diag-whiten}
\end{equation}
However, diagonal normalization only sets the diagonal elements of the sample covariance to unity; it preserves all off-diagonal channel correlations, leaking $92.3\%$ 5-way style identity.

\begin{proposition}[Stiefel Polar Quotient \& Zero 2nd-Order Leakage]
\label{prop:stiefel}
Let $W_k \in \R^{D\times k}$ ($k=32 \ll N$) be an orthogonal basis of dominant semantic tokens. For centered projection $Z = W_k^\top P(I_N - \frac{1}{N}\mathbf{1}\mathbf{1}^\top) \in \R^{k\times N}$ with compact SVD $Z = U \Sigma V^\top$ ($U\in O(k), V^\top V = I_k$), define the Stiefel Polar Quotient:
\begin{equation}
Q_k(x) = \sqrt{N}\, U V^\top \in \R^{k\times N}.
\label{eq:stiefel-q}
\end{equation}
Then for any input $x$, $Q_k(x)$ identically satisfies:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Zero spatial mean: $Q_k(x)\mathbf{1} = \sqrt{N} U(V^\top\mathbf{1}) \equiv \mathbf{0}$;
\item Exact identity Gram matrix: $\frac{1}{N} Q_k(x) Q_k(x)^\top \equiv I_k$;
\item Optimal geometric preservation: $Q_k(x)$ is the unique closest point to $Z$ in Frobenius norm among all matrices satisfying $\frac{1}{N} Q Q^\top = I_k$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}
Evaluating on holdout paintings confirms that while diagonal whitening leaks $92.33\%$ source style through 2nd-order Gram statistics, the Stiefel polar quotient $Q_{32}$ drops 5-way Gram leakage to $\mathbf{20.00\%}$ (exact uniform chance) with numerical error $< 2.3\times 10^{-4}$.

\textbf{2) Complementary Style Fiber $\Pi_\parallel(x)$ \& Universal Chord:}
Conversely, $\Pi_\parallel(x)\in\R^{795}$ collects the exact discarded statistics—perceptual Oklab color moments/quantiles/covariance ($27$-D) plus raw RGB DINOv2 spatial $(\mu_{\mathrm{sp}},\sigma_{\mathrm{sp}})$ ($768$-D). A 2-layer spherical encoder $\phi_\eta:\R^{795}\to\mathbb{S}^{127}$ trained with class barycenters $\{\bar{u}_y\}_{y=1}^K$ defines the \emph{source-label-free universal chord}:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
e(x_s\!\to\!\tau) &= u_\tau - \phi_\eta(\Pi_\parallel(x_s)),\\
u_\tau &= \bar{u}_{y_t}\;\text{(class)} \quad \text{or} \quad \phi_\eta(\Pi_\parallel(x_{\mathrm{ref}}))\;\text{(1-shot)}.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:universal-chord}
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Quotient--Fiber Orthogonality \& Antisymmetry]
\label{prop:antisym}
For any $\Delta x\in\ker(w_Y^\top)$, $\Pi_\perp(x+\Delta x)\equiv\Pi_\perp(x)$ and $\mu_{\mathrm{sp}}(Q_k)\equiv\mathbf{0}$. Under 1-shot self-reference ($x_{\mathrm{ref}}=x_s$), $e(x_s\to x_s)\equiv\mathbf{0}$ identically without style labels, and $e(\tau_1\!\to\!\tau_2)=-e(\tau_2\!\to\!\tau_1)$ preserves chord antisymmetry.
\end{proposition}

\subsection{Operator II: Quotient-Guided Latent Transport ($\mathcal{T}$)}
\label{sec:transport}

Least-squares regression $Y_{64}\approx w_{\mathrm{luma}}^\top z + b_{\mathrm{luma}}$ on training latents yields unit luminance axis $\hat{w}_Y = w_{\mathrm{luma}}/\norm{w_{\mathrm{luma}}}_2 \in \R^4$, splitting $\R^4$ orthogonally into 1-D structural luminance $P_Y = \hat{w}_Y\hat{w}_Y^\top$ and 3-D chrominance $P_C = I_4 - P_Y$. Aligning $P_C z_0$ of wavelet base anchor $z_0=\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{base}}(\mathcal{C}_1(z_s),y_t)$~\cite{daubechies1992ten} toward target chroma moments gives warm-start $z_0^\perp$ with zero change to $P_Y z_0$.

\textbf{1) \method{} (Spatially Routed Real-Time Amortization):}
We distill balanced $(z_0^\perp, z^\star)$ pairs into a $\fastStudentParams$ network $\Delta_\theta$ ($6$ width-$96$ dilated $[1,1,2,2,4,1]$ blocks, $53\times 53$ latent receptive field) using Huber, Haar-HF, and S10-VGG moment losses. Rather than spatially uniform FiLM, a \emph{Semantic-Chord Router} interacts $Q_{32}(u)$ with $e=e(x_s\!\to\!\tau)$ at $16\times 16$ to predict spatially heterogeneous modulation $(\Gamma_{64}, B_{64})$, gated by continuous strength $\lambda$:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\hat{z}_{s\to\tau}(\lambda) &= z_0^\perp + \lambda\,g_{\mathrm{id}}(e)\cdot\Delta_\theta\big(z_0^\perp, P_Y z_s, C_{64}, Q_{32};\, e\big),\\
g_{\mathrm{id}}(e) &= \tanh^2\!\big(\norm{e}_2 / \sigma_e\big).
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:prism-1step}
\end{equation}
When $e=\mathbf{0}$ (e.g., $x_{\mathrm{ref}}=x_s$), $\tanh^2(\norm{e}_2/\sigma_e)=0$ enforces exact $\Delta\equiv\mathbf{0}$ for all $\theta$, preserving 100\% self-identity.

\textbf{2) \methodflow{} (Exemplar Cross-Attention \& Core Density Flow):}
To break the single-mean centroid collapse of class-averaged models without requiring multi-second diffusion inversion, \textbf{\methodflow} formulates a generative bridge directly conditioned on arbitrary reference exemplars $x_{\mathrm{ref}}$. First, to prevent noisy boundary outliers (e.g., 3D museum installation photos) from corrupting the style manifold, we filter training exemplars using intra-training $k$-NN-30 core density. Second, we extract un-normalized reference appearance tokens $V_{\mathrm{ref}} = [\mathrm{PCA}_{128}(P_{\mathrm{RGB}}(x_{\mathrm{ref}})),\,\mathrm{Unfold}_{4\times 4}(z_{\mathrm{ref}})] \in \R^{256\times 192}$ alongside Stiefel semantic keys $K_{\mathrm{ref}} = Q_{32}(x_{\mathrm{ref}})$. Cross-attention layers at $16\times 16$ and $32\times 32$ bottleneck depths allow source semantic queries $Q_{32}(x_s)$ to dynamically retrieve matching exemplar brushstroke tokens without $64\times 64$ source-edge straightjacketing. A split-horizon ODE integrator transitions smoothly from early luminance-locked layout stabilization to late unconstrained brushstroke synthesis, completing in $134\,\text{ms}$ ($7.5\,\text{img/s}$).

\subsection{Operator III: Spectral-Bottleneck Decoding ($\mathcal{D}_\star$)}
\label{sec:s10}

Partitioning the $49.49\text{M}$ SD-VAE decoder into four coupled channel streams, first-order Taylor importance~\cite{molchanov2019importance} $\mathcal{I}(g)=|\sum_{(c,i)\in g} W_{c,i}\partial\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{rec}}/\partial W_{c,i}|$ prunes widths to $[64,128,192,256]$ across five recovery stages, yielding \textbf{\methoddec} ($\decParams$, $-\decPrune$). Beyond $\decSpeedup$ faster inference ($15.6\,\text{ms}$ @ B8) and $\decGain$ higher cross-style Top-1, its $4.7\times$ smaller footprint enables fast BF16 pixel-gradient distillation in Operator~II.

\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Benchmark, Unified Referee, and Dual-Gate Protocol}

We evaluate at $512\times 512$ on five WikiArt movements~\cite{wright2022artfid}: \emph{Early Renaissance} (ER), \emph{Impressionism} (Imp), \emph{Minimalism} (Min), \emph{Rococo} (Roc), and \emph{Ukiyo-e} (Uki). Strictly disjoint from the 4,400 clean training latents and 300 calibration holdout paintings (verified zero stem overlap and minimum latent RMS $L_2=0.3438$), 150 test sources $\times$ 5 targets form \textbf{750 tasks}: \textbf{Cross600} ($600$ off-diagonal transfers) and \textbf{Same150} ($150$ identity controls). Every method is scored in isolated processes by four frozen backbones—\textbf{CSD-ViT-L}~\cite{somepalli2024csd} (5-way \emph{Top-1} and \emph{Margin}), \textbf{DINOv2-small}~\cite{oquab2023dinov2} (\emph{DINO-S}, \emph{DINO-C}), \textbf{CLIP ViT-B/32}~\cite{radford2021clip} (\emph{CLIP-S}, \emph{Affinity}), and \textbf{LPIPS}~\cite{zhang2018unreasonable}—under two falsification gates:

\section{Discussion and Broader Impacts}
\label{sec:discussion}

\textbf{Inference Efficiency, Energy Consumption, and Green AI.}
A critical impediment to democratizing neural style transfer in mobile apps and digital creation suites is computational expenditure. Diffusion-based stylizers such as StyleGallery and StyleShot rely on dozens of sampling steps or gradient updates through gigabyte backbones. On an NVIDIA RTX~4090 ($350\,\text{W}$ TDP), processing one $512\times 512$ image with StyleGallery ($30.4\,\text{s}$) consumes $\sim 2.96\,\text{Wh}$. In stark contrast, single-step \textbf{\method} ($17.0\,\text{ms}$) consumes merely $0.0012\,\text{Wh}$ per image—a $\mathbf{2{,}460\times}$ energy reduction, enabling real-time interactive preview at $58.7\,\text{fps}$ and sustainable edge deployment.

\textbf{Consumer-Grade 12 GB Hardware Compatibility.}
On a 12\,GB NVIDIA RTX~3060 Windows server, \textbf{\method} achieves single-step latency of $28.5\,\text{ms}$ ($35.1\,\text{img/s}$) with $1.50\,\text{M}$ active parameters. Even for the generative regime, \textbf{\methodflow} trains smoothly on consumer hardware under $4.06\,\text{GB}$ peak VRAM under micro-batch accumulation ($32\times 4$), maintaining a safe $66\%$ memory headroom.

\textbf{Theoretical Insights into Feature Entanglement.}
Our analysis demonstrates that \emph{high reconstruction capacity in latent tokenizers is fundamentally antagonistic to controllable cross-domain transport}. When autoencoders and flow models are trained with self-reconstruction objectives, gradient descent exploits high-frequency correlations as shortcut solutions. Standard diagonal whitening fails because it leaves off-diagonal covariance intact, allowing networks to reconstruct $92.3\%$ of source identity from residual Gram patterns. By projecting semantic tokens onto the Stiefel manifold ($Q Q^\top \equiv I_k$), \textbf{\method} eliminates all linear and quadratic style cues, ensuring that spatial queries represent pure invariant geometry.

\textbf{Aesthetic Fidelity across Historical Movements.}
A known failure mode of universal style transfer is ``style flattening''—rendering distinct artistic movements into generic painterly textures. Because \textbf{\method} pairs Stiefel quotient cross-attention with an un-normalized 27-D Oklab color fiber and spectral decoder pruning, it faithfully captures movement-specific physical media: the matte, chalky flat planes of Minimalism, the delicate multi-layer gold leaf and tempera of Early Renaissance panels, the rapid broken impasto of Impressionism, and the crisp woodblock boundary carving of Ukiyo-e prints without color bleeding.

\textbf{Limitations and Future Work.}
While \textbf{\method} handles portraits, architectural scenes, and landscapes reliably, extreme input aspect ratios ($>3:1$) can cause minor anisotropic deformation in the $16\times 16$ cross-attention map. Furthermore, extending Stiefel polar projection to cross-modality synthesis and non-photorealistic video rendering represents a compelling avenue for future exploration.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \textbf{\method}, a unified latent style transfer framework dismantling Source-Style Lock via Orthogonal Quotient--Fiber Duality. By replacing diagonal whitening with the Stiefel Polar Quotient ($Q Q^\top \equiv I_k$), \textbf{\method} achieves exact zero 2nd-order Gram leakage ($20.0\%$) and unlocks source-free chords. Across 750 benchmark tasks, real-time \textbf{\method} ($17.0\,\text{ms}$, $\fastFps$) unconditionally outperforms Seedream~4.5 and StyTr$^2$ across all metrics, while generative \textbf{\methodflow} ($134.1\,\text{ms}$) surpasses StyleGallery in affinity ($0.658$ vs.\ $0.643$) and accuracy ($60.5\%$ vs.\ $52.5\%$) at $226\times$ higher throughput.
